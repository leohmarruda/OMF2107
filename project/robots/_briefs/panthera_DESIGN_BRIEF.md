## 1. Role (kept, because these are mechanics)

- Weight class and size feel: light and small. Low mass means high speed and low armour. A skeleton with little
  protection, so damage hits it hard (fits the `career` regions).
- Range and speed identity: the fastest robot in the roster. Fast walk and jump, quick recovery, short effective range
  that it closes with mobility, not reach. Wins by spacing and repeated entry, not by trading hits.
- Move set: the Jaguar move set is kept for now (decided 2026-11-10), which keeps parity work valid; revisit after the
  M2 parity gate. Suggested for any later change: fast, low-damage strings; one evasive
  special (a dash or leap that changes position); one punisher with real startup so it is not unbeatable.
- Signature special(s), by function only: a repositioning dash (cross-up or retreat); an aerial combo starter.
- Pilot fit: **(you)** which pilot archetypes suit it (the nimble ones). In `career` its low armour makes repair
  decisions harder; check the economy simulation (R07) for a passive "run away" strategy.
- Balance guardrail: agility must not make it untouchable. Stun, range and the repair cost of a fragile frame are the
  checks (parity on `classic`; playtest on `career`).
