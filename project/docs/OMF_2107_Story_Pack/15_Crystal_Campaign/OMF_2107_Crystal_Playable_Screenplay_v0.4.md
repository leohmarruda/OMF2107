# ONE MUST FALL 2107 — CRYSTAL DEVROE: THE OPEN HAND

**Selected-pilot screenplay — six chapters, active continuity revision v0.3 (1 November 2026)**  
**Project baseline:** Story Pack v1.2; this is an *alternative Crystal POV within one save file*, not a second simultaneous tournament.  
**Status:** Updated for v1.5 Devroe case resolution and v1.4 Raven dark-trait canon (Raven enjoys the power of exploiting Crystal; his behavior can be manipulative). Earlier v0.1 preserved as a restrained-branch baseline. New characterization **author-set**: Crystal feels an unbidden, powerful submissive response to Raven, making an intimate D/s dynamic between them feel natural. Its expression remains optional, reciprocal, explicitly agreed, and revocable. Neither attraction nor obedience proves trust. New scene dialogue, evidence details, and the Angel alternative are **draft proposals**, not established history.

## Production contract and route vocabulary

- Crystal (33) and Raven (36) are adults. Crystal is the renowned 2097 champion; Raven is Kreissack's former bodyguard. She has an unresolved relationship with him and cannot overlook his concealed connection to the Devroe shuttle tragedy.
- **Private dynamic:** Crystal is the one who feels compelled, *before conscious choice*, to yield to Raven's calm authority. She can feel her body answer before she likes the answer. The game never interprets this reaction as permission. Raven may offer leadership only after asking, and she may decline, redefine, suspend, or end the dynamic. Her agency in sport, politics, investigation, family, and public affairs is never transferred to him.
- **Relationship choices:** `raven` (conditional D/s romance), `angel` (proposed adult reciprocal romance, only after Raven relationship clearly closed), `friendship`, `single`. Refusal to enact submission does **not** erase Crystal's involuntary attraction, and Raven's moral accountability is required even on a platonic route.
- **Match order on screen:** Selene (`GA2`) then Ari (`GA1`). Tournament engine order is Ari then Selene. Every official fight has exactly one winner and one loser. Default: Crystal wins both, defeats Ren in Q1, loses to Ari at S_AB; championship goes to Ari after he beats Lyra. Alternate outcomes replace those results. The Nova boss happens after the actual final and confers no trophy.
- **Shared event anchors:** gala Day 0; qualifications Days 3–7; QFs and Kreissack biological survival Days 8–10; S_AB Day 11; genuine rescue Day 12 midday; S_CD Day 12 evening; final and Nova confrontation Day 13. The exact hour-by-hour scene calendar remains provisional.
- Essential clues come through public records, independently held evidence, and verified testimony whether or not Crystal wins a fight, romances Raven or Angel, or receives a private confession. Intimacy must not buy exculpatory evidence.

# CHAPTER 1 — THE PERSON INSIDE THE STATUE
**Days 0–1 | Dramatic question: What remains of a hero when everyone owns her image?**

### C1.1 — The champion under glass [MANDATORY]
**INT. JOVIAN ORBITAL ANNIVERSARY GALA — NIGHT**

Above a crowded ceremonial hall, a luminous projection replays Crystal Devroe's 2097 victory: Jaguar closing on Kreissack's formidable machine, the announcer calling the moment a liberation, a city of cheering faces. The projection freezes on Crystal's triumphant pose while the real CRYSTAL, now thirty-three, quietly checks the seam of her formal crimson jacket. Her clothes are immaculate; under the jacket is the compressed strength of someone who never stopped fighting.

**HOST:** Ten years ago, she gave us our future.

A child asks her to autograph a replica Jaguar. Crystal kneels to sign the toy rather than make the child reach for her hand.

**CHILD:** Did you know you were going to win?

**CRYSTAL:** Absolutely not. That's why I had to keep thinking.

A journalist requests a photograph before she's even stood up. She smiles anyway. Beyond the photograph, screens display the achievements of the post-2097 reforms, including glossy Ganymede infrastructure footage. She knows several of those promises proved smaller than their advertising.

`CHOICE — public appearance`: command the camera with practiced glamour / replace the official speech with a modest acknowledgment of unfinished reforms / leave the dais to talk to the repair crews. Alters press and later dialogue, never her tournament advantage.

### C1.2 — Raven in the doorway [MANDATORY]
**INT. BACKSTAGE OBSERVATION GALLERY — CONTINUOUS**

RAVEN stands outside the light, scarred, severe, in black. He has not applauded the archival victory. They shared one brief, difficult affair after 2097; neither has managed to dismiss it as a mistake.

**RAVEN:** That speech got shorter.

**CRYSTAL:** Your congratulations got less charming.

**RAVEN:** I didn't come to congratulate you.

She makes for the exit. He says, very quietly, **“Crystal. Look at me.”**

Her head turns before she consciously decides to turn it. The ease of that response makes her cheeks warm. She dislikes the startling relief she feels when he takes charge of an otherwise unbearable moment; she dislikes that he notices. **Raven notices and privately enjoys the asymmetry.** Whether he exploits it is a separate, consequential decision, not proof that she has agreed. In competition she commands crowds and machines. With this particular man, some private part of her wants to put down the weight. She cannot explain why, and neither can he.

**CRYSTAL:** You don't get to order me around.

Raven steps aside, giving her an open path.

**RAVEN:** Then don't let me.

**CRYSTAL:** That's not the same thing as not wanting to.

He has no clever reply. Neither does she. The attraction is unmistakable, but it does not resolve what he knows about her parents.

`CHOICE — first response`: admit the attraction without promising anything / warn Raven not to mistake a reflex for consent / leave without answering. All three preserve **the inner pull**. Only the later `raven` branch may become an enacted D/s relationship.

### C1.3 — Christian's objection [MANDATORY]
**INT. VETERANS' WALKWAY — NIGHT**

CHRISTIAN DEVROE, her twin, joins her beneath the huge 2097 mural. He saw Raven talking to her.

**CHRISTIAN:** He was with Kreissack when the shuttle records vanished.

**CRYSTAL:** I know who he worked for.

**CHRISTIAN:** Knowing who he worked for isn't knowing what he did.

Christian brings a duplicate of an archived service log. Several entries associated with their parents' shuttle are stamped **“custody transfer complete”**, but the chain of custody contains a blank signature field. He will not say Raven killed their parents. He will say he cannot verify Raven's innocence.

**CHRISTIAN:** I won't make your decisions. I just want the same evidence to count when it's a man you want.

**CRYSTAL:** That's the part I hate about being a reasonable person. You have to be reasonable when it hurts.

`CHOICE — family strategy`: investigate alongside Christian / ask an independent records officer to authenticate the log / keep a separate copy while Christian leads. All create a verified chain independent of Raven.

**[v1.6 Christian parallel scene hook — CH1.1 / CH2.3]** He retains his own civilian preservation request and receives E1 independently by Day 5. If Crystal elects a parallel investigation, their disagreement is real but does not erase the same custody-gap proof. Her romance and publicity preferences remain exclusively hers; his only legitimate request is that both of them use authenticated facts. See `18_Christian_Subplot/OMF_2107_Christian_Independent_Storyline_v0.1.md`.

### C1.4 — The anniversary's second image [MANDATORY]
**INT. RESTRICTED EXHIBITION CORRIDOR — LATER**

An antique Nova insignia flickers on a maintenance console. A technician labels the sighting as a harmless legacy artifact. Crystal has seen too many official “harmless” descriptions to believe this one unexamined. ANGEL watches from another corridor; REN asks a technician a question about interface permissions.

**CRYSTAL (to herself):** Once can be a glitch. Twice is an instruction to look closer.

Crystal stores a timestamped public diagnostic, not privileged neural information. Raven passes the distant doorway; she does not follow him. Beneath the gala's celebration, a sealed life-support deck is active. The audience sees it; Crystal does not yet know who is inside.

**Chapter end:** Crystal receives an invitation to endorse Milano's reform program. A private second message arrives from a sender who claims the Devroe shuttle transfer was never completed. Its provenance is unverified.

**INT. REPAIR GYM — EVENING**

**RAVEN:** Your shoulder's doing the work of your feet.

**CRYSTAL:** My shoulder has more medals.

He moves closer but stops outside her personal space.

**RAVEN:** Want the correction?

She hates how quickly she wants to say yes. **A desire to obey is not a decision to obey.**

`CHOICE — rehearsal`: “Show me, but ask before touching.” / “Tell me; I won't be taking orders.” / “Not from you.”

If she explicitly accepts a demonstration, he guides a wrist only after she extends her arm. His commands are short and precise; her body follows with unnerving readiness. She calls a stop and he stops immediately.

**CRYSTAL:** This is not permission for anything else.

**RAVEN:** I know.

**CRYSTAL:** You'd better.

She leaves with better foot placement, **not better tournament stats**; this is character action and ordinary training available from other coaches. If she refuses, a licensed training technician provides equivalent instruction. Raven watches her depart. Part of him is pleased that a single suggestion from him occupies so much of her attention; **he likes the possibility of leverage**. In a restrained branch he chooses not to use it. # CHAPTER 3 — THE RIGHT KIND OF POWER
**Days 6–7 | Dramatic question: Can she admit what she wants without giving away what she is?**

### C3.1 — Rooftop without armor [OPTIONAL ENSEMBLE FAN SERVICE / COMEDY]
**EXT. OBSERVATION-DECK POOL — SUNSET**

Jupiter hangs over a rooftop pool. Elite athletes are unexpectedly ordinary in swimsuits, old scars and bad jokes. COSSETTE and IBRAHIM calmly ignore the chaos.

Crystal wears a dark red swimsuit under a borrowed wrap, relishing anonymity for ten seconds. Raven is already there in a plain shirt, looking as though someone has threatened him with recreation.

**CRYSTAL:** You dressed for a funeral.

**RAVEN:** I saw the guest list.

They take turns challenging each other to keep a completely straight face through the others' dreadful poolside interviews. An optional private glance can be charged; a friendship branch becomes a rare unguarded laugh. No photographer gets a private image without consent.

### C3.2 — The words neither has said [OPTIONAL RAVEN PATH; CHOICE IS NOT RECIPROCITY]
**INT. QUIET GALLERY, OFF THE POOL — NIGHT**

Crystal comes because she wants an explanation for their unmistakable chemistry, not because Raven has summoned her. A translucent wall looks out over Jupiter. He stands at the far end, leaving her the door.

**CRYSTAL:** You speak and something in me moves before I've decided to move. I hate that you know.

**RAVEN:** I know you dislike it. I don't know what it means unless you tell me.

He enjoys how easily she watches for his reaction. In a **restrained NPC branch**, he refuses to treat that reaction as consent. In a **predatory NPC branch**, he tests whether her attraction can be traded for silence or separation from Christian; this is documented manipulation, and the private-romance gate closes regardless of how she initially responds.

**CRYSTAL:** I think I want to be able to surrender control with you. Not to be controlled everywhere. Just—when I choose to stop holding everything up.

**RAVEN (restrained branch):** Then the choosing matters more than the surrender.

**RAVEN (predatory branch):** You could let me decide which questions hurt you.

**CRYSTAL (predatory branch, after reflection):** I postponed that witness call because you made me doubt my own judgment. The witness left. That's not an invitation. I'm leaving.

In the **predatory branch**, his manipulation can briefly succeed, costing an opportunity to interview a departing witness. The independently preserved shuttle evidence remains available. Once she recognizes the pressure, Crystal leaves, informs Christian or a trusted investigator, and refuses to let the delay become permanent silence. No D/s scene follows. In the **restrained branch**, Raven admits he likes having power over her and wishes to lead privately but, for this moment, does not demand the role; he does not receive intimacy as a reward for saying the right words.

`CHOICE — D/s intention`:

1. **Explore privately:** acknowledge desire for a consensual dominant/submissive dynamic, with negotiated limits, ongoing check-ins and a way to stop immediately. This is a **proposal**, not a commitment or blanket permission.
2. **Acknowledge desire, refuse the relationship:** she can feel submissive and still decide that Raven is too secretive to trust.
3. **Leave the subject open:** no enacted dynamic now; potential later reconsideration, if explicitly chosen.

Only on a reciprocal, **non-coercive restrained** exploration path do they agree that **“Stop” ends any scene immediately; “Slow” means pause and check in**. Consent is situation-specific; no orders concerning tournament outcomes, political statements, family investigation, access credentials, or whether she may leave. She will not accept demands that conceal evidence. Raven can also decline a scene at any time.

**CRYSTAL:** There's one thing you will not decide for me.

**RAVEN:** Name it.

**CRYSTAL:** The truth about my parents.

**RAVEN:** I should have told you sooner.

She moves toward him; he does not close the remaining distance.

**CRYSTAL:** Then tell me. Not tonight if you can't. But until you do, this goes no further.

Even on the exploration path there is no private intimacy that buys forgiveness. The moment can end with a consenting, restrained hand touch—if *both* choose—or with her leaving without it.
