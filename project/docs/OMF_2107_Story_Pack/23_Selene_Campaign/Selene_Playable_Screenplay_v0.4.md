# OMF 2107 — Selene Vale: THE ART OF DECEPTION

**Playable cinematic screenplay, editorial draft v0.1 — Story Pack v2.4**  
**Six chapters, Days 0–30.** Selene is **32**, an independent executive and pilot of **Mirage** whose underlying status as an heiress of a rival robotics conglomerate is concealed behind a legally registered professional vehicle. Her family conglomerate's name, the precise legal alias, and the identity of its controlling executives are intentionally unapproved placeholders; do not fabricate a new canonical dynasty. All characters offered romance are adults. Intimate sequences are optional, suggestive, and cut away before explicit activity.

**Screen/engine fight convention:** The screenplay presents Selene–Ari (GA3) before Selene–Crystal (GA2). The shared simulator orders Group A as GA1 Crystal–Ari (NPC), GA2 Crystal–Selene, GA3 Ari–Selene. Therefore the route adapter MUST translate `(ari_win, crystal_win)` into `(crystal_win, ari_win)`. In the default NPC route Selene loses both and does not qualify. Every match has one winner; circular 1–1–1 *group standings* are not drawn fights. Every subsequent match and opponent comes from the shared model, never a script assumption. NPC alternate-bracket outcomes and Group A circular seeding are provisional.

**Shared world invariants:** The Day-9 discovery proves Kreissack's biological survival but does not prove Nova controls him. Day 12's safety rescue is genuinely beneficial and Nova-initiated, with an apparent Kreissack attribution anomaly. The unique Nova composite core is destroyed on Day 13 when Kreissack uses the retention latch after the player helps break the executive gateway; his final line is **“Apparently, I can still surprise you.”** The Day-30 authentication of Kreissack's 2092 Devroe murder order cannot be revealed earlier. Varga's unauthorized human emotional research, the Ganymede relief obstruction, and Selene's commercial investigations are distinct lines of misconduct. Romance and business choices do not alter fair match results, the independent essential clues, or other characters' consent.
