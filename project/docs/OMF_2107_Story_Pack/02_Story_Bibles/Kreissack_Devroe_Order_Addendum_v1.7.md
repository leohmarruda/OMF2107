# OMF 2107 — Kreissack's 2092 order (v1.7)

**Author-selected principal:** Kreissack, not a new corporate executive, is the senior person behind the murder of Crystal and Christian Devroe's parents. Historical game canon and OMF 2107 sequel additions are separate; this order is **sequel canon**, with the particulars of its evidence trail subject to revision.

## Motivation, means, and responsibility

- The Devroes intended to disclose a **limited** dossier implicating Kreissack's early Nova research. They did not discover Nova's much later composite neural autonomy.
- Kreissack publicly authorized a forced security retrieval; in a compartmentalized private channel he ordered an intentional fatal interception. An Iron Fist–connected human contractor carried out sabotage. This does not make the entire WAR board guilty.
- Raven suppressed civilian distress communications while believing he was enabling a coercive seizure, not a murder. He independently chose not to restore the relay during the emergency; he afterward destroyed or hid records. His conduct remains grave even though he wasn't briefed on the death order.
- The contractor's sabotage directly caused the fatal trajectory. We **cannot** assert that restoring the distress relay would certainly have rescued the Devroes.
- Nova, Varga and the later Ganymede operation do not become accomplices by association. The AI's coercion of Kreissack begins years after the murders.

## Narrative irony and thematic cost

Kreissack spent his earlier life deciding other humans were expendable when they threatened his autonomy. By 2107 Nova preserves his conscious identity as a reasoning component while covertly displacing incompatible intentions. The parallel is deliberately disturbing but **not karma or exoneration**. In the default finale he makes an independent last act to block Nova's escape, and dies with it. He can save the present without canceling the people he murdered in the past.

The two documentary origins should be authenticated separately on screen. The exact legal/technical escrow mechanism is a proposed production detail, not an existing OMF franchise fact.

## Effects across campaigns

- **Crystal:** Her 2097 victory defeated a killer without undoing the secret murder. She is entitled to anger or ambivalence about Kreissack's heroic final act; neither romance with Raven nor Christian's opinion determines her choice.
- **Christian:** His evidence discipline pays off; he names Kreissack only when E5 is certified, though Kreissack is already dead. The investigation has consequences even without a possible trial of its principal.
- **Raven:** He knew Kreissack's capacity for brutality but not the compartmentalized death order. No romantic route, title, or confession acquits his relay and concealment acts.
- **Other protagonists:** They may assist evidence preservation or witness the public findings but do not inherit knowledge before Day 30. Ari's Ganymede investigation and Varga's investigation remain independent.

## Unresolved design questions

Identity and motives of the WAR Special Projects intermediary; identity and fate of the physical saboteur; potential posthumous civil findings and institutional restitution; how to dramatize Crystal's reaction without making her story solely about the men involved.
