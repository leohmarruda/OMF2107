# OMF 2107 — Milano Angston Character Addendum (v2.5)

## Source-established characterization

Milano, **45**, is a wealthy WAR board contender and pilot of **Gargoyle**. Intelligent, patient, persuasive, technically credible and institutionally ambitious, he seeks leadership and a legacy independent of his inherited wealth. He has supported **bounded neural-link research** under negotiated safeguards; he is neither the author of Varga's unauthorized experiments nor knowingly responsible for keeping Kreissack alive. He is implicated in his institution's failures because he trusts clever leaders and formal safeguards too much. He is a reformer who does not naturally want to surrender power.

**Existing relationships:** Selene — sophisticated flirting and competitive corporate maneuvering; Crystal — possible political ally unwilling to lend her face cheaply; Ari — critic of WAR whom Milano may learn to respect. Haldane — technocratic power center, not wholly obedient to the board; Sorell — chair concerned about institutional survival.

## Proposed deepening (not approved canon)

Milano's guiding belief is **“Good leaders can keep bad systems under control.”** His fatal blind spot is that he treats the competence of his own leadership as a partial substitute for enforceable accountability. The fact that he genuinely wants to reform WAR makes the self-deception more interesting. He is disciplined as a Gargoyle pilot; a loss is legitimate and reveals weaknesses that a board vote cannot paper over.

**Core contradiction:** When faced with evidence his original safeguard has been bypassed, he can either authorize an independent authority to judge him or demand more authority for himself. Neither branch changes the history of his first vote.

**New proposed supporting character:** Mira, an adult governance-secretariat aide who is loyal to a verifiable record and refuses personal coverups. She provides information only under plausible access and can resign in the dark route. Her exact name and identity should be approved separately.

**Selene:** Their intellectual and physical attraction can be authentic. Neither is obliged to surrender their corporate ambitions for intimacy. The default is professional rivalry and flirtation, not a relationship; any alternate mutual romance requires independent choices by both and decoupled material power. This is consistent with Selene's route v0.1.

**Ethical alternatives:** `independent_reform` (costly honesty), `managed_reform` (genuine partial progress with residual complicity), or `consolidate` (competent authoritarian-style corporate control with reputational and legal danger). No route lets Milano turn Nova into a private commercial asset or redeem Kreissack's crimes.

**Post-finale:** The unique Nova core is destroyed on Day 13; the Devroe order is authenticated only Day 30. Milano should not become an omniscient exposition character.
