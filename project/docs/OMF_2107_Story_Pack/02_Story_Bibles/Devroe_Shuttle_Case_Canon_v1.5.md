# The Devroe shuttle case — definitive sequel backstory (v1.5)

**Status and precedence.** Approved *direction* from the ongoing request is to resolve Raven's involvement. The factual resolution below is **new working OMF 2107 story canon proposed for author approval**, *not* a claim about historical OMF 2097 game's official canon. It supersedes the **open question** in the current project bible, but not the factual material in `01_Original_Sources`. Earlier v1.3–v1.4 scripts are retained as snapshots; active v1.5 revisions use this resolution. The precise dialogue, invented investigators and paper trail remain editable.

## Decision: Raven helped make the murder possible, knew it was wrong, and hid it

**The victims.** In **2092**, Dr. Devroe and his wife were the only passengers on a civilian shuttle traveling **Luna → Earth**. He had been a senior WAR biogenetics researcher. They were attempting to put a limited dossier on the early, secret Nova research program beyond Kreissack's control. They did **not** understand Nova's eventual 2107 composite-brain autonomy mechanism. The original 2097 DigiLink access code `Nova` remains Crystal's first clue, not a new decoded full explanation.

**The principals.** Human operatives linked to **WAR Special Projects**, acting to protect Kreissack's research, commissioned an off-the-books interception. An **Iron Fist–connected contractor** covertly installed a malicious flight-control command, turning a purported seizure into a lethal operation. This explicitly selects the *earlier derivative-lore possibility* that Iron Fist was implicated; it does **not** pretend this is confirmed in the 1994 manual. Kreissack knowingly authorized coercive recovery of the dossier; whether he signed a direct kill order is **not established** and is unnecessary to prove the attack or Raven's participation. Individual identities/signatures of the flight-code author and the person who ordered lethal escalation are open for a later villain reveal. WAR is not a monolith: the responsible chain is specific people, not every employee.

**Raven's historical acts (unchangeable by the 2107 player):**

1. **Before launch,** age approximately 21 and working for Kreissack's security unit, Raven authorized a timed **suppression of the shuttle's civilian distress relay** under a fraudulent 'protected transfer' designation. He knew the Devroes were aboard, knew a nonconsensual seizure of their research was planned, and accepted the risk to them in exchange for money, standing, and leverage. He **did not know in advance** that the interception contractor had installed a fatal flight-control attack. He did not code or implant that attack.
2. **During the emergency,** Raven saw a verified **crew request to restore emergency communications** while the suppression remained active. He had the authority and a real opportunity to clear the relay hold, but chose **not** to, fearing the unauthorized operation would be exposed. He cannot know whether removing the hold would have saved them; he knew refusing could deprive them of a chance to seek help.
3. **After the deaths,** Raven knowingly moved the authorization and distress record out of civilian investigators' reach, certified a misleading 'custody transfer complete' index and retained a private fragment as future leverage. He later learned the flight-control anomaly was deliberate sabotage. He was not a passive uninformed employee merely carrying an order. Nor did he personally execute the fatal command.

**What killed them.** The contractor's malicious attitude-control override drove the shuttle into an unrecoverable Earth-approach trajectory. The exact component details are treated as forensic exposition, not an action blueprint. The shuttle's genuine fatal deviation and the blocked distress relay are **separate mechanisms**: sabotage is established as the direct cause; Raven's decision deprived the crew of an emergency channel, contributing to the danger without a proven but-for claim that contact would have guaranteed survival. The cover-up was a separate knowing offense. **Nova itself did not act in 2092**, nor did Varga's later experimental program cause the deaths.

## The morally important difference

- **What Raven can say truthfully:** 'I did not plant the flight command, and did not know the interception was a killing operation before launch.'
- **What he cannot plausibly deny:** 'I knowingly enabled a forced seizure. I could have restored the distress channel. I chose secrecy. I falsified the record and concealed later evidence that the deaths were intentional.'
- **No convenient exoneration:** his failure to foresee the precise lethal override is not proof he was innocent; neither is he automatically the direct murderer. A criminal conviction, charge, plea or sentence depends on fictional jurisdiction and adjudication and should **not** be predetermined by romance choices.
- **No forced forgiveness:** Crystal's involuntary submission to Raven remains psychologically authentic, but learning the facts can change her judgment of whether she wants him in her life. Christian has separate standing, grief and choices. A reunion never makes them withdraw testimony.
- **No coercion reframed as D/s:** using the shuttle truth to obtain obedience is exploitation even if Crystal responds involuntarily. Any later romance requires the truth to reach her **independently**, Raven's full sworn admission, independent proceedings that do not depend on their relationship, no unresolved coercive incident, both adults' free choices, and explicitly negotiated, revocable private limits. Even those conditions only permit a possible ending; they don't obligate Crystal to choose it.
- **Raven remains dark on a cooperative route:** he can confess partly to end blackmail or regain leverage, still enjoy power and feel fear of consequences. A confession is accountability, **not** a personality transplant.

## Dramatic payoff and production guardrails

Raven sees his own mentality reflected in Nova's covert veto: both enjoy power exercised without the other person's informed choice. The comparison is thematic, **not** a scientific assertion that Raven controls minds. Crystal's private desire does not solve the violation of her parents' autonomy. In one route, she can desire Raven and refuse him at the same time. In another, she may choose a bounded adult relationship while openly testifying about his wrongdoing. The bittersweet tension is the point; **no late 'one sacrifice' clears the past**.

**Open for later selection, not secret retcons:** names of the human kill-order signatory and saboteur; surviving testimony; which charges are filed; whether a post-hearing romance is a preferred default or merely optional; whether a named Iron Fist leader from another adaptation belongs in this continuity. The **historic mechanism and Raven's three acts above are fixed as this v1.5 working resolution** even if those names change.
