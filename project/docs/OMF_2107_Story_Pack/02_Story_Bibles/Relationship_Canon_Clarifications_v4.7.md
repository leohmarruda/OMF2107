# Relationship canon clarifications — v4.7

**Authority:** Direct author decisions, 2026-11-03. Supersedes inconsistent historical lines and scene proposals; historical files are retained as provenance.

## Dante and Lyra

- They knew each other before the tournament and **develop their romantic relationship early**, before the Day-1 gala.
- Their opening-day relationship is already established, with shared familiarity and affection. It is not a meet-cute or a new connection formed only because of the tournament.
- Lyra may later choose to leave Dante, pursue Steffan, remain with Dante, or become single in alternative branches. Her decisions and Dante's reactions remain separate. Dante's default feelings toward Steffan remain nonromantic; an optional future romance needs later development.

## Crystal and Raven

- Crystal feels a strong involuntary submissive attraction to Raven. Raven knows and enjoys exploiting that vulnerability.
- **Crystal is unaware of Raven's manipulation while it operates, precisely because her submissive response obscures the coercive mechanism.** She can notice feelings, missed opportunities, discomfort or problematic demands without recognizing Raven's calculated exploitation.
- Raven's intent and the harm are objective story-state facts, not determined by Crystal recognizing them. The player may see the manipulation from Raven's POV while Crystal's POV does not.
- Crystal may learn of it **later only if a distinct, evidenced revelation is authored** (independent record, credible testimony, or confession). Do not script spontaneous insight at the end of the same manipulation scene. Whether/when that happens remains a proposed branch, not a fixed canonical revelation.
- Her unawareness is **not consent** and cannot clear coercion history. Raven's predatory branch remains ineligible for romantic-intimacy rewards even if Crystal sincerely believes her compliance voluntary. She retains agency over independent choices and can enforce boundaries without understanding the hidden motive.

## Integration rules

- Separate `raven_manipulation_occurred` (objective) from `crystal_knows_raven_manipulation` (epistemic). The former does not set the latter.
- Do not use Crystal's apparent consent or relationship desire to reset Raven's accountability.
- Do not infer a breakup between Dante and Lyra from early Steffan flirtation alone.
- Scenes that imply Crystal immediately spots the predatory tactic are superseded by this decision; later independently established knowledge of the shuttle case is a **different fact** and is preserved.
