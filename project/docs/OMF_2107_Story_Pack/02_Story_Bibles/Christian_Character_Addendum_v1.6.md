# Christian Devroe — supporting-character addendum v1.6

**Status:** Editorial expansion for the OMF 2107 sequel. Established older lore: Christian is Crystal's 33-year-old twin, a former fighter and independent investigator. The material below defines a proposed richer subplot, not a change to original OMF 2097 canon or to the locked v1.5 2092 shuttle history.

## Character engine

- **Surface:** Protective, impatient, dry humor, forensic discipline; can be irritatingly certain he knows the safest path.
- **Private wound:** His identity became entangled with proving what happened to his parents. Crystal's public heroism leaves him feeling like an appendix to her life, a feeling he dislikes admitting.
- **Want:** A defensible independent record of the 2092 shuttle operation, including Raven's culpability and the responsible human chain.
- **Need:** Learn that care cannot entail ownership of Crystal's future; reclaim a life beyond guarding a family memory.
- **Flaw with consequences:** Trying to prevent Crystal's mistakes may lead him to speak over her or expose her feelings publicly, straining their relationship. The evidence still proceeds independently.
- **Distinct competence:** Safeguards on provenance, secure civilian archives and witness interviews. He is **not** the pilot who defeats Nova or the engineer who decodes its cognitive implementation.
- **Positive texture:** Youthful love of rhythm and instrument repair; secret enjoyment of terrible live music; capable of flirting, friendship and jokes unrelated to murder evidence.

## Independent evidence journey

E1 Day 5 civil custody gap → E2 Day 8 signed relay suppression → E3 Day 10 independently verified lethal flight-command manipulation and contractor link → E4 Day 11 emergency message received and restoration ignored → post-final separate Nova override evidence → weeks-later oversight testimony.

He never invents a causal claim that restoring the distress channel guaranteed survival. Raven's withholding may damage their trust or delay voluntary admissions, but cannot erase E1–E4, which are preserved by independent custodians. Identifying the superior who ordered killing is still a future story question.

## Relationships

**Crystal:** A sister with full legal and romantic agency; she can desire Raven while condemning his acts. Christian's support may mature from surveillance to partnership or may fracture and require repair. Neither the player nor a sympathetic brother gets a veto over her choices.

**Raven:** A man implicated by independent records in their family's tragedy. Christian need not forgive him if Raven assists against Nova, confesses, or dates Crystal. He can insist on public accountability without fabricating additional crimes.

**Leena:** Existing *proposed* optional secondary romantic route (already floated in the relationship branch bible): mutual journalistic respect, a night-market dance, potentially intimate but nonexplicit post-hearing connection. Leena must independently want the relationship, be age-eligible and unattached, and obtain accepted editorial recusal; Ari–Leena/Angel–Leena or any mutually exclusive partner blocks it. The romance is **not canonized** by including it here. Their friendship or professional bond can survive without romance.

**Other allies:** Ari's Ganymede inquiry is independent; Cossette brings technical ethics; Plug helps during evacuation; an anonymous neutral forensic officer preserves records. None serve as hired mouthpieces for Christian.

## Ending constraints

Three autonomous futures are viable: public access archive, continued independent investigations, or stepping away to revive ordinary pleasures. Each may include sibling closeness or distance, and possible romance only when eligible. No career choice determines the trial verdict, whether Crystal remains with Raven, or which tournament fighter wins. A complete solo ending must never be framed as punishment.

**Precedence:** v1.5 Devroe history remains authoritative; v1.6 screenplay and route spec control only Christian's previously thin subplot. New lines in revised Crystal/Raven drafts are optional secondary scene integrations; original versions stay preserved.
