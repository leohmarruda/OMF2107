## Character Bible — Version 1.0

**Setting:** 2107, tenth anniversary of the original Ganymede tournament  
**Genre:** Futuristic fighting game / corporate thriller / ensemble drama  
**Tone:** Glamorous, energetic, seductive, witty, mysterious, occasionally tragic  
**Cast:** 12 playable pilots, returning supporting characters, new antagonists  
**Status:** Working story bible

# 1. Ensemble overview

The cast is designed around four major emotional storylines:

1. **Ari and Mara:** Love, competition, financial desperation, and ideological conflict.
2. **Steffan, Lyra, and Dante:** Seduction, partner poaching, jealousy, revenge, and ambiguous attraction.
3. **Jean-Paul and Varga:** Psychological manipulation, sexual tension, and the denial of genuine feelings.
4. **Crystal and Raven:** An unfinished relationship complicated by guilt, distrust, and secrets from 2097.

These are complemented by political, technological, familial, and platonic relationships.

# 2. Returning playable pilots

## 2.1 Crystal Devroe — The Veteran Heroine

**Age:** 33  
**Public identity:** Legendary winner of the 2097 tournament, independent public figure, symbol of reform.  
**Signature HAR:** Jaguar — a refined championship model with the recognizable original silhouette.

### Appearance and presence

Elegant, athletic, confident, with distinctive dark red hair and a practical but sophisticated wardrobe. Her colors combine deep red, white, and metallic accents. She dresses like someone accustomed to interviews but more comfortable in a training facility.

Her manner is composed, yet she occasionally reveals a mischievous side.

### Personality

- Intelligent, determined, courageous, perceptive.
- Warm toward people she trusts; guarded toward strangers.
- Proud of what she accomplished in 2097 but uncomfortable with being idealized.
- Can become stubborn and controlling when she believes she knows what's best.
- More capable of humor and affection than her public image suggests.

**Emotional need:** To believe that her accomplishments genuinely improved people's lives.

**Central flaw:** She has difficulty accepting that good intentions and courageous actions can still produce inadequate results.

### History and secret

After winning in 2097 and exposing part of Nova's history, Crystal became involved in Ganymede's administration and later withdrew from corporate authority.

She remained a respected independent public figure.

Her most troubling private knowledge is that the reforms following 2097 were weaker than she publicly suggested. She knows some agreements were compromised, although she did not know about the later decision that cost Ari's sibling their life.

### Important relationships

**Raven:** Former brief romantic involvement, unfinished attraction, profound distrust. He possesses information about her parents' deaths that he has never fully disclosed.

**Christian:** Her twin brother. They love each other but disagree about the risks she's willing to take.

**Ari:** Initially sees Crystal as a glamorous symbol of WAR's failed reforms. Their ideological conflict may develop into genuine respect.

**Milano:** An old political ally who wants her endorsement. She distrusts his increasing willingness to compromise.

### Default arc

Crystal returns for the anniversary tournament, initially seeking to protect her legacy and investigate suspicious technological developments.

She gradually realizes that she must confront the limitations of her previous victory rather than defend its reputation.

### Player alternatives

She can renew or reject Raven's relationship, publicly support Ari, join Milano's reform coalition, pursue independent exposure, or prioritize winning the championship.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"You think winning changed everything? I used to think so too."

## 2.2 Raven — The Dangerous Antihero

**Age:** 36  
**Public identity:** Former bodyguard of Kreissack, feared mercenary, unpredictable veteran.  
**Signature HAR:** Katana — aggressive precision, intimidating weaponry, minimal ornamentation.

### Appearance and presence

Tall, lean, imposing, with severe features, dark clothing, visible scars, and an economical style of movement.

His HAR uses dark metallic colors with restrained crimson accents.

### Personality

- Direct, intimidating, pragmatic, cynical.
- Remarkably observant.
- Dislikes sentimental declarations.
- Occasionally displays unexpectedly dry humor.
- Capable of fierce loyalty, but reluctant to trust anyone.

**Emotional need:** To be valued as a person rather than merely as a useful weapon.

**Central flaw:** He treats secrecy and emotional distance as substitutes for independence.

### History and secret

Raven helped conceal information related to the Devroe shuttle operation during his service under Kreissack.

The extent of his responsibility remains an unresolved question, not an automatic absolution. He may have preserved evidence while allowing other records to be destroyed.

His knowledge becomes particularly dangerous when Crystal begins investigating Nova again.

### Important relationships

**Crystal:** Their old attraction returns, but his secrets threaten any possibility of intimacy.

**Mara:** Mutual respect between professional fighters, expressed through sarcasm, competitive challenges, and occasional cooperation.

**Christian:** Christian considers him potentially complicit in the Devroe family tragedy.

**Kreissack:** A former employer whom Raven fears, resents, and knows better than most people.

### Default arc

Raven initially seeks money and independence. As the conspiracy resurfaces, he is forced to choose between preserving his freedom and disclosing information that could destroy his relationship with Crystal.

### Player alternatives

He may confess, expose the conspiracy, sell the evidence, maintain his old loyalties, or attempt to confront Kreissack personally.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"Trust is expensive. You sure you can afford it?"

## 2.3 Jean-Paul — The Charming Spy

**Age:** 37  
**Public identity:** Sophisticated competitor, consultant, socialite, and incorrigible flirt.  
**Signature HAR:** Shadow — deceptive positioning, projections, misdirection.

### Appearance and presence

Elegant, expressive, impeccably dressed. Dark hair, an easy smile, and an apparent absence of anxiety.

He wears luxurious but understated outfits and always appears comfortable in expensive surroundings.

### Personality

- Charming, witty, seductive, socially intelligent.
- Enjoys flirting and psychological games.
- Often treats difficult conversations as entertainment.
- Distrusts authority and grand ideological claims.
- Uses humor to conceal loneliness and insecurity.

**Emotional need:** To experience genuine intimacy without surrendering control.

**Central flaw:** He mistakes the ability to manipulate others for emotional independence.

### History and secret

After 2097, Jean-Paul continued operating between corporate and political intelligence networks.

He enters the anniversary tournament partly to investigate suspicious research funding and obtain information valuable to his contacts.

His hidden dealings could compromise his credibility if exposed.

### Important relationships

**Dr. Varga:** A sophisticated mutual game of seduction, suspicion, psychological observation, and denied feelings.

**Selene:** Professional rivalry and flirtatious competition between skilled social manipulators.

**Milano:** Occasional ally, occasional adversary. Both recognize each other's capacity for deception.

**Crystal:** Respects her but finds her moral seriousness amusing.

### Default arc

Jean-Paul suspects Varga's unofficial research and approaches her intending to gain her confidence.

She recognizes his strategy. Their mutual manipulation becomes an intimate relationship neither wants to acknowledge as genuine.

When he uncovers the unauthorized experiments, he faces the conflict between professional duty, self-preservation, and emotional attachment.

### Player alternatives

He can expose Varga immediately, cooperate in her research, attempt to change her behavior, exploit the information, or withdraw from their relationship.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"Of course I'm telling the truth. I haven't decided which truth yet."

## 2.4 Steffan Thomas — The Celebrity Champion

**Age:** 27  
**Public identity:** Former prodigy, charismatic championship star, media favorite.  
**Signature HAR:** Pyros — spectacular flames, aggression, crowd appeal.

### Appearance and presence

Attractive, athletic, extravagantly stylish. Expressive hair, designer streetwear, fashionable accessories, and a practiced camera smile.

He is visually recognizable even out of uniform.

### Personality

- Charismatic, provocative, funny, flirtatious.
- Highly competitive and somewhat arrogant.
- Enjoys admiration and public attention.
- More emotionally vulnerable than he admits.
- Reacts badly to humiliation or perceived disrespect.

**Emotional need:** To be loved and respected independently of his fame or talent.

**Central flaw:** He treats attention, romantic success, and competitive victory as interchangeable proofs of personal worth.

### History and secret

Steffan has spent ten years becoming a celebrated professional HAR pilot.

His rivalry with Dante began before the anniversary tournament and has grown into a public spectacle.

Steffan privately fears that his popularity may outlast his competitive excellence.

### Important relationships

**Lyra:** He deliberately pursues her while she is involved with Dante. What begins partly as a competitive provocation develops genuine romantic stakes.

**Dante:** Intense rivalry, mutual fascination, wounded pride, and deliberately ambiguous attraction.

**Shirro:** The elderly showman helped shape Steffan's celebrity image and knows how to manipulate his ego.

**Ren:** A younger competitor whose unusual abilities threaten Steffan's status.

### Default arc

Steffan seduces Lyra away from Dante, triggering a scandal and increasingly hostile matches. He beats Lyra in qualifying and Dante in the quarterfinal.

Over time, he must recognize how his pursuit of victory and attention has hurt others: when Lyra confronts him he admits that at first he pursued her partly to humiliate Dante, ends up losing her and the semifinal, and congratulates her sincerely. His regret is real but does not earn Lyra back. His charged rivalry with Dante stays deliberately ambiguous.

### Player alternatives

He can pursue or avoid Lyra, apologize to Dante, intensify the rivalry, confront the ambiguous emotional tension, or focus on defending his championship reputation.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"Don't hate me because I'm better. Hate me because I make it look easy."

## 2.5 Angel — The Mysterious Hacker

**Age:** Provisional 38  
**Public identity:** Reclusive veteran with an obscure background and exceptional technical skills.  
**Signature HAR:** Chronos — temporal manipulation, unconventional positioning, unpredictable movement.

### Appearance and presence

Minimalist, understated, striking without seeking attention. Cool color palette, practical clothing, and a slightly unconventional technological aesthetic.

She often wears equipment of her own design.

### Personality

- Intelligent, independent, introverted.
- Dry, sometimes mischievous humor.
- Highly sensitive to dishonesty.
- Emotionally guarded but capable of deep attachment.
- Dislikes being observed or treated as an enigma.

**Emotional need:** To regain control over her identity and private life.

**Central flaw:** She struggles to distinguish justified caution from habitual isolation.

### History and secret

Angel knows more about earlier neural-interface experiments than she publicly admits.

She has preserved fragments of historical records that contradict WAR's official account of Nova's destruction.

Her exact relationship to earlier experiments remains unresolved and should not duplicate Ren's voluntary participation.

### Important relationships

**Ren:** Mutual intellectual fascination, skepticism, and potential friendship.

**Crystal:** Occasional investigative cooperation, but Angel distrusts Crystal's public exposure.

**Ibrahim:** Professional respect mixed with disagreements about experimental safeguards.

### Default arc

Angel detects unusual patterns in the new neural-link telemetry and connects them to surviving Nova technology.

Her investigation gradually forces her to choose between remaining anonymous and becoming a public witness.

### Player alternatives

She can release evidence, infiltrate the program, protect her anonymity, share information with Ren, or bargain with WAR.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"Someone erased the records. They forgot to erase the mistakes."

## 2.6 Milano Angston — The Corporate Heir

**Age:** 45  
**Public identity:** Influential WAR board contender, wealthy heir, refined political operator.  
**Signature HAR:** Gargoyle — aerial mobility, precision, sophisticated engineering.

### Appearance and presence

Tall, elegant, impeccably groomed. Premium tailored clothing, understated corporate insignia, carefully controlled expressions.

His style projects authority without flamboyance.

### Personality

- Intelligent, diplomatic, patient, ambitious.
- Charismatic in a restrained way.
- Comfortable with power and negotiation.
- Genuinely interested in WAR's institutional future.
- Can rationalize morally compromised decisions as necessary.

**Emotional need:** To establish a legacy independent of his family.

**Central flaw:** He believes sufficiently intelligent leadership can compensate for inadequate accountability.

### History and secret

Milano has become an important contender within WAR's corporate board but has not secured overall control.

He helped approve limited neural-link research under negotiated safeguards.

He suspects that the technocratic faction has exceeded its mandate but underestimates the danger.

### Important relationships

**Selene:** A sophisticated romantic and political chess match between two corporate heirs.

**Crystal:** He seeks her endorsement and resents her reluctance to support his reform agenda.

**Ari:** Political adversary who may become a valuable ally.

**Technocratic faction:** Professional rivals whose ambitions threaten his position.

### Default arc

Milano attempts to assemble a reform coalition while protecting WAR's strategic and financial interests.

He discovers that corporate compromise has allowed dangerous projects to survive.

### Player alternatives

He can pursue genuine oversight, seize control, negotiate with rival corporations, preserve the existing system, or make a dangerous bargain involving Nova.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"Power isn't the problem. It's who gets to exercise it."

# 3. New playable pilots

## 3.1 Ari Solano — The Ganymede Rebel

**Age:** 26  
**Identity:** Partially customizable gender, appearance, and presentation. Default cinematic concept is masculine, but the central biography remains consistent across configurations.  
**Public identity:** Colonial outsider and outspoken critic of WAR.  
**Signature HAR:** Thorn — a rugged, heavily modified model reflecting Ganymede's industrial fighting culture.

### Appearance and presence

Practical frontier clothing, visible signs of living in a working colony, and an individualistic style rather than fashionable corporate branding.

The default silhouette emphasizes agility and determination rather than physical intimidation.

### Personality

- Principled, courageous, resourceful.
- Curious and intelligent.
- Sometimes sarcastic and impulsive.
- Distrustful of powerful institutions.
- Capable of affection and playfulness when emotionally secure.

**Emotional need:** To recover a sense of agency and belonging after losing a sibling.

**Central flaw:** Ari sometimes confuses refusing compromise with remaining morally consistent.

### History and secret

Ari once admired WAR and believed its technological achievements were essential to humanity's future.

During a colonial political crisis, WAR deliberately withheld emergency supplies or evacuation to protect its negotiating position.

Ari's sibling died, and responsibility was concealed.

Ari has obtained partial evidence but not enough to establish who authorized the decision.

### Important relationships

**Mara:** Default slow-burn romance. They become lovers shortly before learning they must face each other in the tournament.

**Crystal:** Initially distrusts the celebrated 2097 champion; possible eventual ally.

**Milano:** Represents the institutional power Ari opposes.

**Selene:** May offer political leverage, resources, or tempting compromises.

### Default arc

Ari enters the championship seeking recognition and access to evidence.

The relationship with Mara gradually becomes a source of happiness and emotional stability.

Their tournament pairing creates a painful conflict because Mara needs the prize to pay medical debts associated with her mother's care.

Meanwhile, WAR offers Ari increasing access to its advanced neural-link program.

Mara strongly opposes participation, particularly as the technology becomes more invasive.

Ari must decide how much independence to sacrifice for influence, technological power, and the possibility of justice.

### Player alternatives

Ari can refuse the research program, participate conditionally, or become deeply involved.

Ari can defeat Mara in a fair match, lose and pursue a different route, or reach a different relationship outcome through player choices.

Romance remains optional in player-directed routes, but the authored default storyline develops Ari and Mara as lovers.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"You taught me to believe in the future. Then you decided whose future mattered."

## 3.2 Lyra Kisaragi — The Idol Champion

**Age:** 26  
**Public identity:** Celebrated media personality and highly skilled HAR competitor.  
**Signature HAR:** Electra — visually spectacular electrical attacks and fast, acrobatic combat.

### Appearance and presence

Stylish, colorful, expressive. Futuristic idol fashion blended with genuine athletic practicality.

Her wardrobe and promotional performances shift between glamour, playfulness, and competitive confidence.

### Personality

- Witty, mischievous, flirtatious.
- Highly socially perceptive.
- Ambitious and hard-working.
- Enjoys provoking reactions.
- Dislikes being underestimated.

**Emotional need:** To be recognized as an autonomous person rather than a carefully manufactured public image.

**Central flaw:** She sometimes mistakes control over attention for control over relationships.

### History and secret

Lyra became famous partly through HAR competition and partly through entertainment media.

Her management team has encouraged romantic publicity and staged rivalries.

She has quietly been negotiating greater control of her brand and career.

### Important relationships

**Dante:** Her established partner at the beginning of the tournament.

**Steffan:** Deliberately pursues her; she develops genuine attraction, contributing to a major scandal.

**Ren:** An affectionate friendship characterized by teasing and unexpected warmth.

**Shirro:** Recognizes her ability to influence public narratives and attempts to recruit her into tournament publicity.

### Default arc

Lyra is, from her first appearance, an elite athlete (three major championships) and a model with a huge following; her skill is stressed over her photogenic fame. She leaves Dante for Steffan, partly because of genuine attraction and partly because she enjoys exercising romantic freedom, and owns the injury to Dante.

The public scandal increasingly reduces her identity to the two men's rivalry.

After Jean-Paul shows her Varga's manipulation and Steffan admits his initial motive to humiliate Dante, she recognizes his affection may be sincere but refuses to be a trophy in a competition she never joined, and ends the relationship.

She legitimately defeats Steffan in the semifinal (using what she learned from her qualifying loss to him), reaches the championship against Ari in the default timeline, and keeps her own sporting career and brand. She does not automatically reconcile with either man.

### Player alternatives

She may stay with Dante, pursue Steffan, reject both, expose media manipulation, or use her influence to protect another pilot.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"Boys, if you're finished arguing over me, I'd like to win my match."

## 3.3 Dante Vey — The Passionate Rival

**Age:** 28  
**Public identity:** Explosive young champion and Steffan's principal competitor.  
**Signature HAR:** Shredder — rapid offense, punishing close-range pressure, aggressive mobility.

### Appearance and presence

Athletic, striking, slightly rebellious. Fashionable but less polished than Steffan.

His clothes and manner suggest someone who enjoys taking up space and challenging authority.

### Personality

- Passionate, proud, competitive.
- Sarcastic and provocative.
- Intensely loyal when secure.
- Easily wounded by rejection or humiliation.
- Capable of sincerity, but uncomfortable displaying vulnerability.

**Emotional need:** Recognition and affection that are not contingent on defeating someone else.

**Central flaw:** He often turns emotional pain into competition or revenge.

### History and secret

Dante has spent years competing against Steffan professionally.

His admiration, envy, resentment, and fascination are difficult even for him to disentangle.

His relationship with Lyra initially offers stability, although their ambitions sometimes conflict.

### Important relationships

**Lyra:** His established partner, later drawn toward Steffan.

**Steffan:** Principal rival. Their fixation on each other contains deliberately ambiguous undertones of attraction and attachment.

**Mara:** Respects her willingness to fight without corporate privileges.

### Default arc

Steffan's pursuit of Lyra humiliates Dante, who becomes increasingly obsessed with revenge.

Their eventual fight is emotionally charged, and his fixation on Steffan begins to exceed the immediate romantic dispute.

Dante must choose whether he wants to defeat Steffan, be acknowledged by him, or escape the rivalry altogether.

### Player alternatives

He can seek revenge, reconcile, redirect his ambition, pursue another relationship, or confront his confusing emotional attachment.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"You didn't beat me. You just found something else to steal."

## 3.4 Selene Vale — The Undercover Heiress

**Age:** 32  
**Public identity:** Independent executive and professional HAR competitor.  
**True identity:** Heiress to a rival robotics conglomerate.  
**Signature HAR:** Mirage — a new technical model emphasizing feints, optical decoys, and deceptive movement.

### Appearance and presence

Elegant, attractive, sophisticated, with controlled expressions and a mischievous smile.

She can move comfortably between luxurious corporate receptions and high-pressure technical environments.

### Personality

- Clever, ambitious, witty.
- Seductive and socially confident.
- Enjoys competition and manipulation.
- Rarely sentimental in public.
- More rebellious than her polished appearance suggests.

**Emotional need:** To be respected for her personal achievements rather than her family's wealth.

**Central flaw:** She treats vulnerability as a weakness in negotiations.

### History and secret

Selene enters the championship under a constructed professional identity.

Her real goal is to obtain technological and commercial advantages over WAR while building an independent power base.

Her family's corporation is a competitor, but her interests do not always align with theirs.

### Important relationships

**Milano:** Flirtatious corporate rivalry, mutual respect, possible intimacy.

**Jean-Paul:** Espionage competition and playful verbal duels.

**Ari:** Potential political partner whose idealism she alternately admires and exploits.

### Default arc

Selene infiltrates WAR's technological ecosystem and gradually discovers a political conflict more dangerous than expected.

Her true identity is exposed, forcing her to decide whether to defend her family's interests or pursue an independent strategy.

### Player alternatives

She can reveal herself early, bargain with Milano, acquire technology, betray her employers, or help expose the conspiracy.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"Darling, if I wanted to deceive you, you'd be thanking me for the privilege."

## 3.5 Ren Akiyama — The Idealistic Ace

**Age:** 25  
**Public identity:** Exceptional young pilot and voluntary participant in advanced neural-integration research.  
**Signature HAR:** Helix — new experimental HAR designed for unusually precise synchronization.

### Appearance and presence

Androgynous, elegant, restrained. Sleek technical clothing, clean silhouettes, minimal decoration.

Ren's visual identity should appear futuristic without becoming inhuman.

### Personality

- Disciplined, analytical, curious.
- Quietly ambitious.
- Dry sense of humor.
- Occasionally socially awkward.
- Sincerely enthusiastic about technological progress.

**Emotional need:** To demonstrate that personal excellence and scientific advancement can coexist with meaningful human connection.

**Central flaw:** Ren increasingly measures self-worth by technological performance.

### History and secret

Ren voluntarily participates in advanced synchronization experiments to help develop human-machine integration and become a superior pilot.

Ren has experienced unexpected neurological effects but concealed some early results out of fear that participation would be suspended.

The symptoms are real, but Ren does not initially understand their connection to surviving Nova technology.

### Important relationships

**Angel:** Mutual technical curiosity and growing trust.

**Lyra:** A warm, playful friendship that helps Ren engage socially.

**Varga:** Professional admiration, gradually complicated by suspicion.

**Steffan:** Celebrity veteran whose competitive achievements Ren hopes to surpass.

### Default arc

Ren achieves exceptional neural synchronization and becomes a centerpiece of WAR's technological demonstrations.

As the research progresses, Ren must confront the possibility that their pursuit of excellence is compromising their autonomy.

### Player alternatives

Ren can withdraw, demand informed safeguards, continue experimental enhancement, cooperate with Angel, or help redesign the technology.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"The machine doesn't make me less human. Unless I let it decide what human means."

## 3.6 Mara Voss — The Underground Champion

**Age:** 34  
**Public identity:** Former underground HAR fighter, feared mercenary competitor.  
**Signature HAR:** Flail — heavy chains, brutal close combat, industrial durability.

### Appearance and presence

Athletic, rugged, short dark hair, visible signs of a hard-working life.

Worn leather, practical equipment, and an irreverent style contrast with the glamorous competitors.

Her robot is visibly repaired and modified rather than pristine.

### Personality

- Blunt, sarcastic, fearless.
- Funny, casually flirtatious.
- Pragmatic about money and survival.
- Fiercely loyal.
- Uncomfortable with pity and emotional dependence.

**Emotional need:** To protect the people she loves without sacrificing her own independence.

**Central flaw:** She believes accepting help can create obligations that undermine autonomy.

### History and secret

Mara became a respected fighter through underground competitions.

Her mother's medical treatment and ongoing care have left Mara with substantial debt.

She enters the anniversary championship because the prize money could secure her mother's care and eliminate the debt.

Her situation is independent of Nova and the main conspiracy.

She conceals the seriousness of the debt, partly from pride and partly because she does not want her family treated as a publicity opportunity.

### Important relationships

**Ari:** Begins with teasing and mutual irritation, progresses through friendship into a genuine romantic relationship.

**Raven:** Professional rivalry and dry humor.

**Dante:** Mutual respect as fighters who resent corporate privilege.

**Her mother:** A central emotional relationship that should exist beyond medical bills and tragedy.

### Default arc

Mara develops feelings for Ari and gradually allows herself to become vulnerable.

In the default route they become lovers shortly before discovering they will meet in the quarterfinal. (Since Repair Pack v0.2 the player can instead choose to remain friends in Chapter 3; the friendship is not punished and Mara does not become vindictive.)

She refuses to accept a deliberately thrown fight, despite desperately needing the prize money.

When Ari considers joining WAR's advanced neural-link program, Mara strongly opposes the decision.

Her conflict is not simply romantic jealousy. She fears the corporation will exploit Ari's grief and ambitions.

### Player alternatives

Mara can win the championship, lose gracefully, seek other sources of financial independence, preserve the romance, end it over incompatible choices, or pursue a completely independent path.

**Signature dialogue:**  
"I don't need you to save me. I need you to stop doing stupid things trying."

# 4. Principal supporting characters

## 4.1 Dr. Ilse Varga — The Charming Experimenter

**Age:** Approximately 51, provisional  
**Role:** Senior neural-interface scientist; principal psychological antagonist.

Brilliant, charming, attractive, elegant, dryly humorous, and emotionally cold.

She directs legitimate research into human-machine synchronization, but privately conducts unauthorized experiments manipulating the pilots' intimate relationships.

She is fascinated by attraction, jealousy, humiliation, betrayal, protectiveness, and attachment as neurophysiological variables.

Her intellectual curiosity overlaps with voyeuristic gratification and a desire to control other people's emotional lives.

**Principal relationship:** Jean-Paul.

Their mutually manipulative involvement develops genuine attraction, which Varga persistently denies.

Her ultimate crisis occurs when she must decide whether to preserve her research, protect Jean-Paul, or acknowledge that her methods have caused serious harm.

**Important distinction:** Varga's unauthorized emotional experiments are not equivalent to WAR's official neural-link program. The technocratic faction may be guilty of different abuses without knowing about her private project.

## 4.2 Major Hans Kreissack — The Living Nova

**Age:** 113  
**Role:** Hidden antagonist and potential final boss.

His biological brain survived the destruction of the original Nova project.

Kreissack now exists within an advanced robotic life-support system integrated into Nova.

The technocratic faction believes it can exploit his knowledge and technological capabilities.

He cooperates while secretly preparing to betray it.

His primary motivations remain survival, power, autonomy, and the refusal to become obsolete.

He is neither an ordinary AI nor merely a helpless prisoner.

**Narrative reveal:** Clues early; survival confirmed around the midpoint; major confrontation near the finale.

**Combat role:** Modified Nova, potentially with route-dependent configurations or support systems.

---

## 4.3 Christian Devroe — The Protective Brother

**Age:** 33  
**Role:** Crystal's twin brother, former competitor, independent investigator or security specialist.

Christian remains intense and protective, but has matured considerably since 2097.

He distrusts Raven and suspects the official account of their parents' deaths remains incomplete.

He is concerned that Crystal is becoming entangled in the same dangerous circumstances as before.

His storyline explores the difference between loyalty and control.

He can support Crystal, cooperate with Ari, or pursue an investigation independently.

## 4.4 Cossette Akira — The Ethical Engineer

**Age:** 49  
**Role:** Respected HAR designer, former competitor, technical adviser.

Cossette remains a central figure in HAR engineering and the development of Electra.

Her paralysis following the earlier arena accident remains part of her life, without defining her entire identity.

She challenges the assumptions underlying the new neural-link program and advocates informed consent, independent oversight, and safer engineering.

Her relationship with Ibrahim provides mature affection, technical disagreement, and emotional warmth.

She may mentor Ren or help Angel investigate questionable research.

---

## 4.5 Ibrahim — The Machine Enthusiast

**Age:** 58  
**Role:** Veteran HAR engineer and leading human-machine integration specialist.

Passionate about machines, intellectually curious, occasionally socially oblivious.

He sees genuine promise in neural integration, especially its possible medical benefits and the prospect of extending life beyond biological limitations.

He is sympathetic to Ren's ambitions but increasingly uncomfortable with the technocratic faction's methods.

His attraction to Nova's technological possibilities gives him a genuine ethical dilemma.

---

## 4.6 Shirro — The Legendary Showman

**Age:** 83  
**Role:** Tournament founder, veteran announcer, media personality.

Flamboyant, funny, vain, charismatic, relentlessly theatrical.

Shirro returns to celebrate the tournament's anniversary and ensure the spectacle is unforgettable.

He has helped manufacture public rivalries and occasionally concealed embarrassing incidents for ratings.

He has a particularly entertaining relationship with Steffan, whose celebrity he helped cultivate.

Shirro can provide comic relief, theatrical announcements, and eventually an important decision about whether to broadcast a damaging revelation.

## 4.7 Imani Sorell — Chair of WAR's Board

**Age:** Approximately 59, proposed new character  
**Role:** Principal corporate authority.

An accomplished executive who prioritizes stability, public legitimacy, investor confidence, and the defense contract.

She does not necessarily endorse the technocratic faction's most dangerous ambitions, but has tolerated secrecy and weak oversight to preserve WAR's position.

She represents institutional complicity rather than an uncomplicated villain.

Her political position is threatened by Milano, Ari's revelations, and Kreissack's eventual betrayal.

---

## 4.8 Dr. Celia Haldane — The Technocratic Director

**Age:** Approximately 55, proposed new character  
**Role:** Board representative supervising advanced neural research.

A brilliant, disciplined technocrat who regards human-machine integration as a transformative breakthrough.

She collaborates secretly with Kreissack, believing his dependence on WAR's infrastructure makes him controllable.

She supports the official experimental HAR program but does not authorize Varga's clandestine manipulation of interpersonal relationships.

Her mistake is believing that scientific competence guarantees political control over the technology.

Their default arc concerns journalistic independence versus corporate pressure.

## 4.10 Leena Sato — The Investigative Reporter

**Age:** To be finalized

# 5. Narrative rules

## 5.1 Character autonomy

Each pilot has a default story when controlled by the game, but the player can redirect their own character's decisions.

Default romances, betrayals, alliances, and sacrifices are not unavoidable player outcomes.

## 5.2 Sexual and romantic content

Attraction, teasing, seduction, jealousy, infidelity, and intimacy are integral parts of the ensemble drama.

The tone is mature and suggestive rather than explicitly sexual.

Romantic developments require adult characters and consensual participation. Manipulative behavior can occur within the fiction without being presented as admirable or consequence-free.

## 5.3 Relationship diversity

The story includes romance, friendship, family loyalty, professional respect, rivalry, and genuine ideological disagreement.

Not every close relationship is romantic, and not every important relationship ends in betrayal.

## 5.4 Gameplay primacy

The story supplies emotional stakes and occasional unusual encounters.

It does not require extensive dialogue to access the core fighting experience.

The arcade mode uses condensed narrative scenes. The cinematic mode expands relationships, mysteries, and branching decisions.

## 5.5 Fighting fairness

Story choices may alter opponents, match circumstances, or selectable equipment, but should not secretly degrade combat controls.

Special fights must preserve readable mechanics and a fair competitive experience.

## 5.6 Multiple conclusions

The tournament winner, political outcome, romantic relationships, survival of evidence, and fate of Nova can vary by route.

There is no requirement that a single character achieve every revelation or resolve every conspiracy.
