# OMF 2107 — v1.7 integration overview

**Direction selected by author:** Kreissack is the senior decision-maker behind the 2092 Devroe shuttle murders, not a newly invented WAR executive. His **individual human authorization** must not be confused with Nova's later composite agency.

## The change

- Kreissack ordered an assassination disguised as a security retrieval, through WAR Special Projects and an Iron Fist–connected contractor. The contractor planted the deliberate fatal flight-control sabotage.
- Raven was **not** briefed on the kill authorization. His prelaunch distress blackout, knowing failure to restore it during the emergency, and postevent concealment remain fixed and reprehensible.
- Nova's 2107 covert domination of Kreissack begins years later and does not excuse his earlier acts. His default death while stopping Nova benefits people but does **not** absolve his 2092 crimes.
- **E5 Day 30:** separate independently archived records authenticate Kreissack's lethal directive. E1–E4 do not name the killer. Christian and Crystal do not learn the authenticated principal until *after* the main finale; not a sudden memory extracted from Nova or Raven.
- WAR's Ganymede actions, Varga's misconduct and Nova's 2107 plan remain separate causal strands.
