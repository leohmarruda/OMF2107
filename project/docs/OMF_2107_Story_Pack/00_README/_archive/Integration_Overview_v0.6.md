## Key continuity repairs

- Ari/Mara can choose romance or friendship, and both are respected in Chapters 3, 4 and 6. Their first kiss is not duplicated by the earlier sparring scene; their doorway choice no longer assumes overnight intimacy.
- Lyra's storyline remains grounded in her athletic career and enormous fanbase. Her default sequence with Dante and Steffan, and her fair semifinal victory over Steffan, are unaffected. The suggested Lyra/Alex romance is possible only **after** Steffan's breakup and completion/recusal from relevant tournament coverage.
- Leena does not trade favorable reporting for intimacy with Ari. Her possible romance is postponed until the Ganymede evidence is independently safeguarded and her professional conflict removed.
- Mara may seek an independent technical partnership with Plug whether or not either has romantic interest. Her financial problems cannot be fixed by dating a mechanic.
- Crystal's attraction to Raven cannot erase his concealment or Christian's independent investigation.
- Official fights remain decisive, with no draws. Romantic choices cannot change combat winners, access to essential evidence, neural-link treatment, or the final boss.
