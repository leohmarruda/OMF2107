# OMF 2107 — v1.4 integration overview

**Date:** 2026-11-01  
**Main addition:** Raven's complete six-chapter playable campaign *The Price of Control*, with author-confirmed morally dark characterization and an authentic predatory pleasure in exploiting Crystal's spontaneous submissive response.

## Core story changes

1. **Raven's moral ambiguity is genuine.** Raven enjoys psychological leverage and the particular thrill of Crystal's involuntary deference. He does not automatically become benevolent when romanced. Player choices determine whether he acts on this impulse, how he handles the Devroe shuttle evidence, and what consequences follow.
2. **Crystal's autonomous decisions remain separate.** Her involuntary desire persists, but she may refuse, report manipulation, demand accountability, or independently choose a narrow, consensual, adult D/s relationship. Predatory conduct is **not** rebranded as consent; manipulation paths do not yield an erotic reward or automatic reconciliation.
3. **Raven's independent campaign** covers professional pressure, corruption, the Devroe shuttle, two Group B fights, conditional QF/SF/final, Nova/Kreissack investigation, genuine rescue and covert-control finale. A defeated Raven still reaches the shared crisis via evidence and escorted assistance.
4. **Romance branches:** Crystal (strictly conditional); proposed Mara (independent mutual attraction and no outstanding commitments); friendship/solo. Raven's actual matchmaking cannot be decided without the NPC's autonomous agreement.
5. **Crystal POV continuity corrected:** active screenplay v0.2 replaces Raven's universally considerate lines with restrained and predatory NPC alternatives. Her route model adds an exploitation-incident gate; original v0.1 remains in the archive for provenance.
6. **Canonical precedence:** `02_Story_Bibles/Raven_Canon_Addendum_v1.4.md` supersedes earlier blanket assertions that Raven necessarily respects boundaries. The Crystal–Raven attraction and a **possible** consensual D/s dynamic remain established. Specific acts of coercion, legal outcomes and new romance pairings are proposals.
