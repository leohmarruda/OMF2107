## Core story

Milano is a capable WAR board contender who genuinely wants institutional reform, but considers intelligent leadership an adequate substitute for independent accountability. He approved limited neural research **under safeguards**, and a faction used that permission as cover for later unapproved activities. His independent investigation follows **governance authorizations, absent safety observers and waiver records**, not Selene's supplier anomaly or the later neural proof uncovered by Ren/Angel.

The player can choose independent oversight and recusal; narrower compromise to retain political capital; or a self-serving bid for extraordinary board authority. These outcomes never retroactively change his earlier vote or the true Nova history. His established flirtatious rivalry with Selene becomes **an optional romance proposal**, only with independent mutual choice, availability, conflict-of-interest separation and absence of coercive bargaining. Solo and professional endings are fully authored.
