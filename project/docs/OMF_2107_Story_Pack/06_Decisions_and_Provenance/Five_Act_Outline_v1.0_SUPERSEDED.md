# ONE MUST FALL 2107
## Cinematic Story Mode — Five-Act Outline v1.0

**Status:** Proposed default route, not an immutable canon outcome  
**Principal viewpoint:** Ari Solano  
**Setting:** WAR's tenth-anniversary championship, 2107  
**Structure:** Prologue, five acts, epilogue  
**Core experience:** HAR combat, celebrity drama, corporate intrigue, romance, and the return of Nova


> **Historical document (kept deliberately).** The default bracket in this v1.0 outline is **superseded**: Group D (Angel wins), the quarterfinals Steffan–Dante and Lyra–Angel, the semifinal Lyra over Steffan, and the final Ari–Lyra are defined in Five_Act_Outline_v2.0.md and Master_Story_Bible_v2.1.md. Also superseded: the fast Ari/Mara romance with no friendship option, and the absence of hidden AI executive control.

---

# 1. Central premise

Ten years after Crystal Devroe won the original tournament and defeated Major Hans Kreissack, World Aeronautics and Robotics hosts a spectacular anniversary championship.

The tournament is simultaneously:

- A prestigious HAR sporting event.
- A demonstration of next-generation military and industrial robotics.
- A means of influencing a lucrative Ganymede defense contract.
- An opportunity for WAR's competing factions to increase their influence.

Twelve pilots compete, including six veterans and six newcomers.

Among them is Ari Solano, a Ganymede colonist whose sibling died after WAR deliberately withheld emergency supplies during a political crisis.

Ari wants to win, gain access to corporate records, and expose those responsible.

Meanwhile, a technocratic faction secretly collaborates with Kreissack, whose biological brain survived inside Nova.

Dr. Ilse Varga, a senior neural scientist, runs a separate unauthorized experiment. She manipulates emotionally charged situations between pilots, studying the effects of attraction, jealousy, betrayal, attachment, and rivalry on neural synchronization.

No single faction completely controls the tournament's consequences.

**Central dramatic question:** What will these pilots sacrifice for victory, love, justice, power, or freedom?

# 2. Tournament format

## 2.1 Qualifying groups

Four groups of three pilots. Each pilot fights two qualifying matches; the top two advance.

| Group A | Group B | Group C | Group D |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crystal | Mara | Steffan | Dante |
| Ari | Ren | Lyra | Angel |
| Selene | Raven | Milano | Jean-Paul |

Group placement is publicly justified by rankings and seeding.

Privately, the broadcast team has promoted potentially profitable rivalries. Varga has provided unofficial recommendations based on predicted emotional responses, but does not control individual fight results.

## 2.2 Default qualifying results

| # | Match | Winner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ari vs Selene | Ari |
| 2 | Mara vs Raven | Mara |
| 3 | Steffan vs Milano | Steffan |
| 4 | Dante vs Jean-Paul | Dante |
| 5 | Crystal vs Selene | Crystal |
| 6 | Ren vs Raven | Ren |
| 7 | Lyra vs Milano | Lyra |
| 8 | Angel vs Jean-Paul | Angel |
| 9 | Ari vs Crystal | Crystal |
| 10 | Mara vs Ren | Mara |
| 11 | Steffan vs Lyra | Steffan |
| 12 | Dante vs Angel | Dante |

The default standings are:

- A1 Crystal; A2 Ari
- B1 Mara; B2 Ren
- C1 Steffan; C2 Lyra
- D1 Dante; D2 Angel

Eliminated pilots remain active in the narrative, particularly Jean-Paul, Selene, Raven, and Milano.

## 2.3 Default knockout bracket

| Stage | Match | Winner |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterfinal 1 | Crystal vs Ren | Crystal |
| Quarterfinal 2 | Ari vs Mara | Ari |
| Quarterfinal 3 | Steffan vs Angel | Steffan |
| Quarterfinal 4 | Dante vs Lyra | Dante |
| Semifinal 1 | Ari vs Crystal | Ari |
| Semifinal 2 | Steffan vs Dante | Steffan |
| Championship final | Ari vs Steffan | Ari |
| Special final encounter | Ari vs Nova / Kreissack | Ari |

This produces six fights for Ari's winning story: two qualifiers, one quarterfinal, one semifinal, the championship final, and Nova.

The special Nova encounter is **not part of the sporting bracket**. It arises from the finale's technological crisis.

# 3. Prologue — The Anniversary Gala

**Day 0**

### Scene P1 — A New Era

A cinematic montage introduces Ganymede, the orbital colonies, modern HAR combat, and the cultural legacy of Crystal's victory in 2097.

Archival footage shows Kreissack's defeat and the reported destruction of Nova.

The footage ends abruptly before the original laboratory's final moments.

A contemporary broadcast celebrates the tenth anniversary and introduces the twelve competitors.

### Scene P2 — Welcome to WAR

The gala is held aboard a spectacular orbital complex overlooking Jupiter.

The atmosphere is luxurious and theatrical: music, fashion, celebrities, holographic advertisements, journalists, and enormous HAR displays.

Shirro welcomes the returning champions.

Crystal receives a standing ovation.

Steffan enjoys the attention.

Dante looks irritated by his rival's prominence.

Lyra playfully teases them both.

Selene quietly observes the executives.

Milano navigates political conversations with the board.

Ren is fascinated by the experimental equipment.

Mara enjoys the expensive food and drinks.

Ari looks uncomfortable among the wealthy guests.

### Scene P3 — Ari Meets Mara

Ari overhears executives discussing Ganymede as an investment opportunity.

Mara notices Ari's irritation and begins teasing.

Their first conversation is sarcastic, competitive, and unexpectedly enjoyable.

Mara initially assumes Ari is just another idealistic protester. Ari assumes Mara is an opportunistic mercenary.

Neither knows how important the other will become.

### Scene P4 — Three Dangerous Encounters

Three short interactions establish the major romantic plots.

**Crystal and Raven:** They encounter each other for the first time in years. Their conversation is polite but charged with unfinished intimacy and resentment.

**Steffan, Lyra, and Dante:** Steffan openly flirts with Lyra. Dante responds competitively. Lyra enjoys the banter but notices the hostility.

**Jean-Paul and Varga:** Jean-Paul recognizes discrepancies in Varga's professional biography. She recognizes his interest is not purely romantic. Both pretend otherwise.

### Scene P5 — The First Anomaly

Ren inspects an experimental HAR interface.

For a moment, a diagnostic display shows an obsolete Nova-related identifier.

The technician dismisses it as a legacy compatibility artifact.

Later, Varga reviews footage of the gala. Instead of concentrating on machinery, she studies the pilots' emotional reactions.

She makes a private note about the Steffan–Dante–Lyra triangle.

**Prologue closing image:** Deep beneath the facility, an unidentified life-support system becomes active.

# 4. Act I — The Spectacle

**Days 1–2**

**Dominant emotions:** Excitement, humor, ambition, attraction.

### Scene I-1 — Opening Ceremony

Shirro introduces the competitors and celebrates the history of HAR combat.

The new championship prize, commercial sponsorships, and defense-contract implications are explained briefly.

The broadcast introduces a public synchronization statistic intended to demonstrate the new link technology.

Varga watches the responses.

### Fight 1 — Ari vs Selene

Ari's first opponent is the sophisticated corporate agent Selene.

Selene attempts psychological provocation, implying that Ari is seeking fame rather than justice.

Ari wins in the default route.

After the fight, Selene offers congratulations and suggests that political influence often requires uncomfortable partnerships.

### Fight 2 — Mara vs Raven

Mara defeats Raven in a brutal but legitimate contest.

The fight establishes her as a serious championship contender.

Raven respects her competence. Their post-fight exchange is dry and amusing.

Ari watches from the stands and begins to reconsider Mara.

### Scene I-2 — The First Invitation

Mara invites Ari to join her for a meal after training.

Ari discovers that she is more thoughtful than her public image suggests.

She refuses to discuss why she needs the prize money.

### Fight 3 — Steffan vs Milano

Steffan defeats Milano in a flashy fight.

Milano takes the loss gracefully and uses the media attention to promote WAR's upcoming defense initiatives.

Steffan uses his victory interview to provoke Dante.

### Fight 4 — Dante vs Jean-Paul

Dante defeats Jean-Paul.

Jean-Paul treats the loss with amusement and continues gathering information.

Dante becomes irritated when journalists ask more questions about Steffan than about his victory.

### Scene I-3 — The Scientist's Interest

Varga compares neural responses from the first matches.

She observes that intense personal emotions can coincide with unusual synchronization patterns.

Rather than proposing a formally reviewed study, she begins secretly arranging social provocations and collecting unauthorized data.

The audience sees that the experiment is deliberate, but not its full extent.

**Act I ending:** Ari receives an anonymous message suggesting that WAR's Ganymede relief records were altered.

# 5. Act II — Games of Desire

**Days 3–7**

**Dominant emotions:** Flirtation, excitement, attraction, suspicion, jealousy.

### Fight 5 — Crystal vs Selene

Crystal defeats Selene.

Selene uses the encounter to investigate Crystal's historical relationship with WAR.

Afterward, Milano recognizes a clue to Selene's concealed corporate identity.

### Fight 6 — Ren vs Raven

Ren defeats Raven through exceptional technical precision.

During the fight, Ren's synchronization briefly reaches a level outside the expected range.

Varga becomes intensely interested.

Ren privately experiences an unfamiliar sensation that cannot be explained by ordinary combat feedback.

### Scene II-1 — Ari and Mara Grow Closer

Ari and Mara train together.

Mara is amused by Ari's seriousness. Ari begins to appreciate her humor and competence.

A quiet conversation reveals that Mara is fighting to pay debts accumulated through her mother's medical care.

Ari describes losing a sibling during the Ganymede crisis.

Their connection becomes more personal.

### Fight 7 — Lyra vs Milano

Lyra wins a spectacular match.

Steffan praises her on air, partly to provoke Dante.

Lyra enjoys Steffan's attention, although she remains involved with Dante.

### Scene II-2 — Jean-Paul Enters the Laboratory

Jean-Paul visits Varga under the pretense of discussing neural-link recovery and performance.

Their conversation becomes a contest of insinuation and charm.

He attempts to obtain information about her research.

She allows him limited access, partly to observe his behavior.

Their mutual attraction begins to become inconvenient.

### Fight 8 — Angel vs Jean-Paul

Angel defeats Jean-Paul.

She detects suspicious telemetry modifications during the match.

Jean-Paul notices her reaction and later approaches her.

They exchange partial information without fully trusting one another.

### Scene II-3 — The First Betrayal

Steffan privately approaches Lyra after a promotional event.

Their flirtation becomes more direct.

Lyra acknowledges her attraction but is initially reluctant to betray Dante.

Steffan admits that antagonizing Dante is part of the appeal, although he also genuinely desires Lyra.

They kiss before Lyra has ended her relationship with Dante.

### Fight 9 — Ari vs Crystal

Ari publicly challenges Crystal's legacy and WAR's failed reforms.

Crystal responds that she opposed the corporation's abuses but could not control everything that followed her victory.

The match becomes politically charged.

**Default result:** Crystal wins.

Afterward, she privately approaches Ari rather than humiliating the younger pilot.

She offers to review the Ganymede evidence.

### Fight 10 — Mara vs Ren

Mara defeats Ren.

Ren's performance is technically impressive but increasingly affected by an unusual synchronization response.

Mara notices something strange and reports it to the technical staff.

Varga classifies the incident as noncritical.

### Fight 11 — Steffan vs Lyra

Steffan wins a glamorous and closely contested match.

Their chemistry is obvious to spectators.

Afterward, Lyra tells Dante she is ending their relationship and acknowledges her involvement with Steffan.

Dante reacts with disbelief and wounded pride.

The confrontation is private, but a selectively edited recording soon reaches the press.

### Fight 12 — Dante vs Angel

Dante defeats Angel.

His performance is aggressive and emotionally charged.

Varga records an unusually strong neural response.

Angel detects a connection between the synchronization analysis and unauthorized telemetry collection.

### Scene II-4 — Ari and Mara Become Lovers

After the qualifying stage, Ari and Mara spend an evening together away from the tournament's publicity.

The scene combines teasing, tenderness, and mutual vulnerability.

Their relationship becomes romantic and physically intimate.

Neither has yet seen the knockout bracket.

They know they might eventually face each other, but neither expects it to happen so soon.

### Scene II-5 — The Scandal Breaks

The media publishes evidence of Steffan and Lyra's relationship.

Dante becomes the object of public speculation and ridicule.

Steffan initially enjoys winning the publicity battle, but Lyra is increasingly angered by being treated as a prize.

Shirro recognizes the ratings potential.

Varga studies the emotional responses with unusual fascination.

**Act II ending:** The qualifying results are finalized. An invitation to participate in WAR's advanced neural-link program reaches Ari.

# 6. Act III — The Ghost in the Machine

**Days 8–10**

**Dominant emotions:** Anxiety, betrayal, surprise, moral conflict.

### Scene III-1 — The Knockout Draw

The quarterfinal pairings are announced.

Ari and Mara discover they must fight.

Their playful relationship is suddenly complicated by the knowledge that only one can advance.

Mara insists Ari must fight seriously.

Ari learns more about the financial consequences of Mara's possible defeat.

### Scene III-2 — WAR's Offer

The technocratic faction offers Ari limited participation in an advanced synchronization program.

The offer includes access to technical resources, valuable contacts, and potentially information related to Ganymede.

Ari considers accepting to advance the investigation.

Mara strongly opposes it.

Her anger is partly ideological but also deeply personal: she fears that WAR will use Ari's grief to make him dependent.

**Branching Point 1 — Neural Program**

- Refuse participation.
- Accept limited participation under conditions.
- Enter the program more deeply.

**Default:** Ari accepts a limited arrangement, believing it can provide access to evidence. Mara is hurt and angry.

### Fight 13 — Crystal vs Ren

Crystal defeats Ren.

Ren displays extraordinary synchronization, followed by a neurological anomaly.

Angel recovers a diagnostic trace associated with old Nova architecture.

She contacts Crystal.

### Fight 14 — Ari vs Mara

The tournament's first major emotional climax.

The pre-fight exchange is intimate, tense, and restrained.

Mara rejects any suggestion that Ari should deliberately lose.

**Default result:** Ari wins a legitimate, difficult fight.

Mara is disappointed, especially because of her mother's medical debt, but she respects the victory.

Their relationship remains strained by Ari's participation in WAR's research program.

**Branching Point 2 — Love and Competition**

The fight outcome is determined by gameplay.

The player's previous behavior influences the post-fight relationship.

Victory does not automatically destroy intimacy, and deliberately throwing a match is not required to preserve love.

### Scene III-3 — The Nova Revelation

Angel, Crystal, and other investigators recover evidence that the Nova system was never completely destroyed.

A preserved biological neural core has remained active for years.

The system contains Major Hans Kreissack's living brain.

The revelation reaches the player privately before becoming public.

The technocratic faction's relationship with Nova is exposed to a small group of characters.

Kreissack is still apparently dependent on WAR's infrastructure.

### Fight 15 — Steffan vs Angel

Steffan defeats Angel.

Angel is distracted by the urgency of her investigation but remains a formidable opponent.

Steffan advances toward a possible confrontation with Dante.

### Fight 16 — Dante vs Lyra

Dante and Lyra meet in a legitimate tournament match after their breakup.

The media sensationalizes the encounter.

Varga clandestinely arranges additional emotional provocations.

**Default result:** Dante wins.

Lyra is furious at the exploitation of their private lives but accepts the sporting outcome.

Dante's victory does not resolve his feelings toward Lyra or Steffan.

### Scene III-4 — The Scientist's Hidden Files

Jean-Paul obtains partial access to Varga's unauthorized experiment.

He discovers that intimate situations and rivalries have been manipulated, monitored, and correlated with neural data.

He realizes Varga has also been studying their own developing relationship.

When confronted, she offers a scientific explanation for their intimacy.

Jean-Paul recognizes that she is concealing genuine feelings behind technical language.

**Act III ending:** Kreissack speaks privately to a technocratic director through Nova's interface.

He promises cooperation while secretly preparing an independent operation.

# 7. Act IV — Fractures

**Days 11–12**

**Dominant emotions:** Anger, heartbreak, suspicion, reconciliation, determination.

### Scene IV-1 — The Political Evidence

Ari, Crystal, and Selene uncover stronger evidence concerning the Ganymede emergency.

The records establish that senior WAR officials deliberately delayed relief to preserve corporate leverage.

The decision contributed to Ari's sibling's death.

Milano recognizes that exposing the documents could destabilize WAR and the defense negotiations.

He argues for a controlled transition.

Ari distrusts another proposal to conceal the truth temporarily.

### Fight 17 — Ari vs Crystal

The semifinal becomes a symbolic rematch.

Crystal is the legendary champion who once represented hope for WAR's reform.

Ari represents the generation that suffered when those reforms failed.

**Default result:** Ari wins.

Crystal publicly acknowledges Ari's achievement and privately offers support.

Their relationship moves toward mutual respect.

### Scene IV-2 — Jean-Paul Confronts Varga

Jean-Paul confronts Varga with evidence of her unauthorized emotional research.

She initially attempts to control the conversation through charm, scientific arguments, and selective honesty.

When he threatens to expose her, she reacts more personally than intended.

Their affair has become a vulnerability for both.

**Branching Point 3 — Varga's Research**

On appropriate routes, the player can:

- Expose the experiment publicly.
- Obtain evidence and postpone disclosure.
- Negotiate her cooperation while preserving accountability.
- Conceal or exploit the research, with consequential risks.

The default ensemble path moves toward exposure, but Varga's access credentials and scientific knowledge may still be essential during the finale.

### Fight 18 — Steffan vs Dante

The second major personal climax.

Steffan and Dante meet in the semifinals.

Their rivalry now encompasses professional envy, romantic betrayal, humiliation, fascination, and ambiguous attraction.

Their pre-fight confrontation is unusually intimate and hostile.

**Default result:** Steffan wins.

Dante's defeat does not neatly resolve their relationship.

Afterward, Steffan has an opportunity to show unexpected consideration for his rival, complicating the audience's interpretation of their feelings.

### Scene IV-3 — The Technocratic Betrayal

The technocratic faction prepares to demonstrate a new neural-command architecture at the championship finale.

The system incorporates technology derived from Nova.

Kreissack covertly acquires access to systems the researchers believed were compartmentalized.

He has been cooperating with them to obtain control over the infrastructure required for his independence.

**Act IV ending:** Kreissack activates a hidden capability within Nova. The system begins issuing commands that the researchers did not authorize.

# 8. Act V — One Must Fall

**Day 13**

**Dominant emotions:** Spectacle, fear, determination, triumph, uncertainty.

### Scene V-1 — The Championship Final

The stadium is packed.

Ari faces Steffan, the veteran celebrity champion.

Their contrasting public images become part of the event:

- Ari, the outspoken colonial outsider.
- Steffan, the established star of HAR combat.

Mara watches from the competitors' area.

Crystal, Milano, Jean-Paul, Angel, and the other pilots follow the match while separate confrontations unfold around the facility.

### Fight 19 — Ari vs Steffan

A conventional championship fight with full spectacle.

**Default result:** Ari wins.

The victory gives Ari money, recognition, and a powerful public platform.

It also places Ari directly in the path of WAR's technological demonstration.

### Scene V-2 — Nova Emerges

The technocratic faction activates its demonstration infrastructure.

Kreissack betrays his collaborators.

Nova overrides its containment architecture and assumes direct control of its robotic systems.

The audience learns that Kreissack survived the events of 2097.

He presents himself not merely as a survivor, but as proof that WAR's technology can transcend biological mortality.

His actual objectives are to secure autonomy, prevent his disconnection, and regain strategic power.

### Scene V-3 — The Emergency Alliance

The remaining pilots and supporting cast respond according to their loyalties.

Angel attempts to isolate the compromised systems.

Ren helps identify the neural-interface vulnerabilities.

Crystal and Christian confront the legacy of 2097.

Milano attempts to prevent the corporation's complete collapse.

Varga possesses knowledge that could help stop Nova, but disclosure of that knowledge also exposes her own wrongdoing.

Mara tries to keep Ari from accepting a dangerous experimental connection merely to gain a combat advantage.

### Fight 20 — Ari vs Nova

The final boss fight.

Nova is physically imposing and mechanically distinctive, with a combination of original capabilities and new technological systems.

The fight must be challenging but fair.

Depending on prior decisions, Nova may use different auxiliary systems or combat configurations.

Possible variations include:

- **Core Nova:** A direct confrontation with Kreissack's robotic body.
- **Network Nova:** Additional remote-controlled units or support attacks.
- **Adaptive Nova:** A version with limited adaptation based on recorded pilot behavior.
- **Overload Nova:** A more unstable configuration connected to excessive neural integration.

These are variants of the same central boss rather than four unrelated final antagonists.

### Scene V-4 — The Final Decision

After Nova is defeated or contained, the surviving evidence and the championship's political consequences remain.

The player faces decisions about:

- What to reveal about the Ganymede crisis.
- Whether to expose Varga's experiments.
- Whether to remain involved with WAR's neural technology.
- Whether to preserve, contain, or destroy Nova's remaining infrastructure.
- Which political coalition to support or oppose.

The choices affect the ending, but do not erase the consequences of earlier actions.

# 9. Epilogue — After the Arena

The default ending is cautiously hopeful rather than perfectly happy.

Ari's victory strengthens the case for investigating the Ganymede crisis.

Crystal publicly supports greater accountability.

WAR's leadership faces scrutiny, but corporate power does not disappear overnight.

The research program is suspended or placed under independent investigation.

Kreissack's ultimate condition remains dependent on the final confrontation and how Nova is handled.

Varga's research is exposed, though she may still cooperate in mitigating the damage.

Steffan, Lyra, and Dante are left with unresolved personal consequences.

Jean-Paul must decide what his feelings for Varga mean after discovering her abuses.

Ari and Mara's relationship depends on the choices made during the tournament, not simply on whether Ari defeated her.

Mara's medical debt remains a serious material problem unless a credible solution is established.

The conclusion should feel like an ending to the championship and a beginning for the characters' changed lives.

# 10. Branching model

The cinematic story tracks four primary dimensions.

| Variable | What it represents | Important consequences |
|---|---|---|
| **Relationship** | Trust and intimacy with relevant characters | Romance, reconciliation, estrangement, support |
| **WAR Involvement** | Degree of participation in neural research | Access, risks, special encounters, final-boss variants |
| **Evidence** | What the player discovers and preserves | Exposure, accountability, possible political outcomes |
| **Alliances** | Which people and factions the player supports | Assistance during the finale, epilogues, selected opponents |

These dimensions should remain partially independent.

For instance, Ari can defeat Mara and maintain their relationship, reject WAR and fail to expose the Ganymede cover-up, or cooperate with WAR and still preserve important evidence.

A player's selected pilot changes the viewpoint and available personal decisions. Critical plot functions remain accessible through alternate sources when an NPC refuses, loses, withdraws, or takes a different path.

The tournament structure can adapt to different winners without making every default character development mandatory.

# 11. Arcade mode adaptation

Arcade mode preserves the main fighting-game rhythm.

For Ari, the suggested six-fight sequence is:

1. Selene — introduction to corporate competition.
2. Crystal — ideological rivalry.
3. Mara — romantic and financial stakes.
4. Crystal — semifinal rematch.
5. Steffan — championship.
6. Nova — special final boss.

The expanded story scenes become short pre-fight exchanges, brief post-fight scenes, and occasional cinematic revelations.

Other pilots receive their own compact introductions, rival encounters, final-boss sequences, and endings.

The cinematic mode contains the fuller ensemble drama and player-directed decisions.

# 12. Outstanding narrative problems

Before writing the full screenplay, the following require refinement:

1. **The Ganymede crisis:** Determine the specific political dispute, relief operation, responsible decision-makers, and documentary evidence.
2. **Nova's technology:** Establish a consistent explanation for Kreissack's survival, technological limitations, and eventual betrayal.
3. **Varga's experiment:** Define precisely what she measures, what she manipulates, and how her research differs from the official program.
4. **Romantic pacing:** Give Ari and Mara enough development before they become lovers; avoid making Lyra's story purely a competition between men.
5. **The aftermath of Mara's defeat:** Treat her mother's medical care and financial situation seriously, without reducing the relationship to charity or rescue.
6. **Alternate protagonist routes:** Ensure eliminated pilots and alternate winners still have complete storylines, without requiring an entirely separate campaign for each character.
7. **Finale pacing:** Prevent the political reveal, Varga exposure, championship final, and Nova boss fight from overwhelming one another.

These should be resolved through a combination of scene writing, fight pacing, and limited playtesting.
