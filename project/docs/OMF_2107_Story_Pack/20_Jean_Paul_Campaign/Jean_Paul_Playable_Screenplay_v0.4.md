# ONE MUST FALL 2107 — Jean-Paul: THE OBSERVER EFFECT

**Selected-pilot screenplay — editorial draft v0.1 (2 November 2026)**  
**Six chapters / Days 0–13, with a Day-30 optional epilogue**  
**Playable:** Jean-Paul, age 37; Shadow HAR. **Core relationship:** Dr. Ilse Varga, adult. **Provisional alternative:** Selene Vale, age 32. **Other outcomes:** friendship and solitude.

**Canon and branch rules.** Jean-Paul's default official matches are Group D losses to Angel and Dante, followed by tournament elimination. Alternative wins must be supplied by the player's actual match results. Jean-Paul is a talented but ethically compromised independent intelligence broker; Varga privately manipulates social encounters and gathers emotional/neural data without valid consent. The attraction between them is real, not artificially manufactured. Neither Varga nor Jean-Paul rigs a fight or controls anyone's affections. Varga's investigation is distinct from Haldane's official HAR program and Nova's secret executive veto. Independently preserved evidence remains available in every route. The post-tournament Nova crisis is **not** an official title match. The audience may see restricted cutaways unavailable to Jean-Paul, as labeled. Kreissack's 2092 assassination order is verified only on Day 30; no one in this campaign knows it as a proven fact earlier.

**Implementation convention:** `JP-Cx.y` labels identify scenes, not chronological days. Group input order is Angel (`GD2`), then Dante (`GD3`), matching the engine. Romance choices never change fight outcomes. Screens may contain conditional text, but never a false fight, title, or confession.

**CHOICE JP-C1-A — approach:**

No choice grants restricted medical records. Even if he refuses, the unauthorized research can be uncovered through other witnesses.
