# OMF 2107 — Twelve-campaign narrative quality and integration audit v2.6

**Basis:** Story Pack v2.5 (12 playable pilots; 268 baseline `unittest` methods). **Nature:** source-based editorial review + focused production repair, *not* human playtesting. **Status:** findings are recommendations, not automatic new canon. **Labels:** (+) observed directly in archive, (~) editorial judgment/uncertainty, (-) speculative possibility. **Source references:** relative filenames and screenplay scene IDs within this archive; no external sources were used.

## Executive determination

**(+) The project has a coherent global canon and a workable route prototype, but it is not yet a production-ready branching screenplay.** Twelve distinct character *premises* exist; tournament matches are correctly separated from relationship choices; the Nova/Kreissack rescue, veto, destruction and Day-30 Devroe order have clear chronology. The 268 passing baseline tests establish many useful *logical* invariants. They do **not** establish convincing dialogue, complete NPC reactions, authored fallback knockouts, credible emergency blocking, full global save-state, or acceptable playtime.

**(~) Major narrative risk:** The campaigns can feel like twelve different investigators collecting their assigned clue on the way to the same boss. This is fixable without replacing the conspiracy or reducing the roster. The objective should be twelve different *decisions*, not twelve times the same exposition.

## Concrete editorial changes recommended for the next script pass

1. **One scene, one causal pivot:** Retain each campaign's strongest protagonist-specific confrontation. Move generic exposition to short shared cutaways; no compulsory full scientific lecture in every POV.
2. **Make both success and loss dramatic:** Every pilot should have a visually different route after group exit and after a quarterfinal elimination. Reuse *locations*, not dialogue that implies a nonexistent match.
3. **Choose three character-specific consequences:** In each campaign script mark one early choice that affects a Day 8–12 scene, one relationship or alliance choice that affects a later conversation, and one ending decision. Produce these three variants **before** adding new love interests.
4. **Require a nonromantic emotional payoff:** The best relationship arc may be trust, rivalry or dignified separation; all twelve need one emotionally complete nonromantic outcome.
5. **Use acts, not encyclopedic briefings:** Required clarity: what is dangerous, what the pilot can do, why they have access, why Nova can be damaged, and what Kreissack chooses. Defer deep philosophical explanation to optional records.
6. **Restore levity in the mandatory path:** A 20–60 second light beat with an identifiable character voice is more valuable than a disconnected swimsuit montage if the rest is all investigation.
7. **Preserve dark traits without glamorizing coercion as consent:** Crystal's involuntary susceptibility to Raven is internal characterization; private dominance needs negotiated consent, and his historical exploitation remains real. Steffan's controlling behavior remains a problem even when Lyra is attracted to him.
8. **Treat consent, romance, fights, and essential evidence as separate state machines:** A fighter can be champion and single; rejected by a lover and still rescue spectators; eliminated and still expose corrupt policies.

## Reused scenes and economy

**(~) Editorial production proposal:** Separate 12 campaigns into **shared event assets** (gala, official bracket transitions, Day 12 rescue, finale mechanism, Day 30 postcredit reveal) and **pilot POV overlays** (unique dialogue, choices, evidence and personal outcomes). At most one physically distinct world-history version per save. Variation should be earned through different protagonist *access and intervention*, not an altered Nova sacrifice. Shared assets need independent ownership and consistency tests. Recommended story-mode targets are in `Arcade_and_Cinematic_Production_Plan_v2.6.md`; these are budgets to test, not measured runtime.

## Why existing 268 passing tests are not a narrative-quality grade

Baseline `run_all_tests.py` discovers `unittest` methods in 18 directories. Many methods loop over 32 binary fight patterns, so **268 is a method count, not 268 separate authored scene playthroughs**. The tests largely exercise routing, flag exclusivity and invariant chronology; they do not perform voice-acting reads, simulate NPC personalities, verify all textual scene gates, assess emotional plausibility or trace global romantic commitments in a single saved story. This release adds a small structural linter to prevent duplicate active scene IDs, but not a substitute for dramaturgical review.
