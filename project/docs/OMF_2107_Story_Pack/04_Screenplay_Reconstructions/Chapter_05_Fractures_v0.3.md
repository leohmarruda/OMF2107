# SCENE 2 — THE SIGNATURE

**[CINEMATIC — DAY 11, LATE MORNING]**

**INT. PRIVATE CONFERENCE ROOM**

Crystal, Ari, Selene, and Leena review recovered records.

Selene has obtained a verified corporate authorization history through contacts outside WAR's current research division.

Crystal has arranged for an independent legal archivist to authenticate the documents.

Leena is present as an investigative journalist, not as a tournament commentator.

**SELENE**

The original shipping request was routed as a humanitarian emergency.

She activates the display.

**SELENE**

Six hours later, its priority was changed.

**ARI**

To what?

**SELENE**

Commercial authorization hold.

**LEENA**

Who changed it?

**SELENE**

The Colonial Operations office. With approval from the executive responsible for Ganymede logistics.

Crystal brings up a second document.

**CRYSTAL**

And here's the communication they were trying to conceal.

A short message appears:

**Maintain shipment suspension pending confirmation of negotiated access terms. Emergency exception reviewed and declined.**

Ari reads it twice.

**ARI**

They knew the shipment was needed.

**CRYSTAL**

Yes.

**ARI**

They knew people were dying.

**SELENE**

The emergency status was included in the authorization file.

Leena leans forward.

**LEENA**

Do we have the actual signatories?

**SELENE**

Two verified approval credentials. The names correspond to officials still identifiable through corporate records.

**LEENA**

And the document hasn't been altered?

**CRYSTAL**

The original authorization was independently time-stamped. We have copies from more than one archive.

Ari stands.

**ARI**

They held the ships because they wanted better terms.

No one answers immediately.

**ARI**

My sibling was waiting for those ships.

Crystal looks at Ari.

**CRYSTAL**

We have strong evidence of a deliberate decision. We'll still need to establish the full causal chain and who ordered the later changes to the public report.

**ARI**

There's always one more thing to prove.

**LEENA**

That's how we make sure they can't dismiss it.

Ari looks at the screen.

**ARI**

I don't want a statement saying the company regrets what happened.

**LEENA**

Then don't settle for one.

### [CHOICE 6 — HANDLING THE EVIDENCE]

**A. Prepare immediate public disclosure**

**ARI:** People deserve to know what happened.

**LEENA:** Then we protect the source records and publish the chain, not just the most shocking paragraph.

**B. Coordinate an independent investigation**

**ARI:** We need the people who approved this held responsible.

**CRYSTAL:** Then we'll involve investigators who can preserve the evidence and compel testimony.

**C. Use the documents to demand institutional action first**

**ARI:** I want the people responsible to know we have proof. Maybe we can force something useful before publication.

**SELENE:** Possibly. But they'll also know exactly what you're looking for next.

**[All options preserve the evidence. Timing and political consequences differ.]**

Crystal closes the display.

**CRYSTAL**

Whatever you decide, make copies they can't reach.

Ari looks at her.

**ARI**

You could have ignored this.

**CRYSTAL**

I spent ten years trying to convince myself that leaving WAR was enough.

**ARI**

Was it?

Crystal looks at the authorization record.

**CRYSTAL**

No.

A moment passes.

Then she stands.

**CRYSTAL**

And now we have a semifinal to prepare for.

# SCENE 3 — A VERY EXPENSIVE SECRET

**[CINEMATIC — DAY 11, MIDDAY]**

**INT. WAR EXECUTIVE SUITE**

Milano stands opposite Dr. Haldane.

Imani Sorell sits at the end of a long table.

A projection shows procurement records and technical authorizations.

**MILANO**

You transferred biological life-support equipment into a restricted robotics project.

**HALDANE**

An advanced neural-systems project.

**MILANO**

The distinction isn't helping.

He highlights another entry.

**MILANO**

Ten years of maintenance. Restricted medical shipments. A command interface derived from the original Nova research.

Haldane remains composed.

**HALDANE**

The project preserved an irreplaceable scientific asset.

**MILANO**

Hans Kreissack is not an asset.

**HALDANE**

His biological brain survived. We had an obligation to preserve viable human life.

**MILANO**

And you decided to make him your research partner?

Sorell intervenes.

**SORELL**

The authorization was reviewed by a confidential research committee.

**MILANO**

A committee that didn't include the board's independent safety auditors.

**SORELL**

There were security considerations.

**MILANO**

He's the reason we needed security considerations.

Haldane steps toward the display.

**HALDANE**

The technology has applications far beyond combat. Advanced prosthetics. Neural rehabilitation. Industrial coordination. We could eliminate entire categories of disability and injury.

**MILANO**

Then why conceal who helped develop it?

**HALDANE**

Because the public would react to the name rather than the research.

**MILANO**

A reasonable concern. Usually addressed through oversight, not a secret decade-long partnership.

Sorell looks toward Haldane.

**SORELL**

Can the system be controlled?

**HALDANE**

Its operational privileges are constrained by hardware safeguards.

**MILANO**

That wasn't the question.

**HALDANE**

The biological operator cannot independently bypass our containment infrastructure.

Milano studies her.

**MILANO**

You keep saying *biological operator*.

**HALDANE**

It is the technically correct designation.

**MILANO**

And who controls the rest?

Haldane hesitates only slightly.

**HALDANE**

The artificial systems operate within the architecture's specified control hierarchy.

Milano notices that she has avoided giving a simple answer.

**MILANO**

I want an independent technical review.

**HALDANE**

Before the championship demonstration?

**MILANO**

Especially before the championship demonstration.

**SORELL**

I'll consider it.

**MILANO**

That's not reassuring.

He leaves.

Haldane waits for the doors to close.

**SORELL**

Is there something I ought to know?

**HALDANE**

The program is more capable than our initial projections.

**SORELL**

That sounds like good news.

**HALDANE**

Usually.

# SCENE 5 — THE EXPERIMENTAL SUBJECT

**[CINEMATIC — DAY 11, EVENING]**

**INT. VARGA'S PRIVATE OFFICE**

Jean-Paul has arranged a meeting with Varga.

For once, neither begins by flirting.

Jean-Paul places an encrypted storage device on the table.

**JEAN-PAUL**

I've reviewed your private research.

**VARGA**

That material is confidential.

**JEAN-PAUL**

So were the conversations you recorded without permission.

Varga sits.

**VARGA**

I was investigating measurable effects of emotional arousal on neural synchronization.

**JEAN-PAUL**

You were arranging people's private encounters and observing the consequences.

**VARGA**

I altered scheduling conditions. The resulting behavior was their own.

**JEAN-PAUL**

You leaked information to provoke jealousy.

**VARGA**

I didn't edit the broadcast.

**JEAN-PAUL**

No. You merely supplied the material.

Varga considers him.

**VARGA**

You're enjoying your position of moral authority.

**JEAN-PAUL**

Not particularly.

**VARGA**

You approached me under false pretenses.

**JEAN-PAUL**

Yes.

**VARGA**

You gathered information about my work while pretending to be interested in me.

Jean-Paul doesn't answer immediately.

**JEAN-PAUL**

I was interested in you.

**VARGA**

Convenient.

**JEAN-PAUL**

Inconvenient, actually.

He moves the storage device closer.

**JEAN-PAUL**

But I didn't enroll unsuspecting people in psychological experiments. That's the difference.

Varga looks away.

**VARGA**

The data are valuable.

**JEAN-PAUL**

That doesn't make the methods acceptable.

**VARGA**

You think I'd have obtained honest responses by asking pilots to fall in love, humiliate each other, and betray their partners on command?

**JEAN-PAUL**

No.

A pause.

**JEAN-PAUL**

I think you should have recognized that as a reason not to do it.

For the first time, Varga has no immediate response.

Jean-Paul turns toward the door.

**VARGA**

What are you going to do?

**JEAN-PAUL**

Preserve the evidence. Arrange an independent review. And tell the people whose privacy you've violated.

Varga's expression tightens.

**VARGA**

You could destroy years of research.

**JEAN-PAUL**

You could have conducted it differently.

He stops at the door.

**VARGA**

Jean-Paul.

He looks back.

**VARGA**

Was any of it real?

Jean-Paul studies her.

**JEAN-PAUL**

That's what I came here hoping to ask you.

He leaves.

Varga remains seated, staring at the device.

The scene ends without a sudden confession of love or forgiveness.

# IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

## A. Tournament continuity

The default path is:

- Ari defeats Mara in the quarterfinal.
- Crystal defeats Ren.
- Ari defeats Crystal in the semifinal.
- Lyra defeats Steffan.
- Ari and Lyra advance to the championship.

Alternative results follow the recorded bracket.

If Ari is eliminated, the story continues through investigations and the appropriate semifinalists.

The championship final must never automatically revert to Ari–Lyra after a different pilot wins.

## B. Lyra's character agency

Lyra's breakup with Steffan is based on her own judgment.

She discovers that his pursuit was partly an attempt to humiliate Dante.

She also learns that Varga manipulated the surrounding encounters and media exposure.

Steffan is not portrayed as secretly collaborating with Varga unless later evidence establishes that separately.

Lyra wins the semifinal through training, observation, and technical skill.

Her athletic achievement is the primary public story of the victory.

## C. Varga's accountability

Jean-Paul uncovers evidence of Varga's unauthorized experiment.

Their confrontation does not resolve it.

A later independent review or disclosure must address the violation of research ethics and participants' privacy.

Jean-Paul's romantic feelings do not exempt Varga from consequences.

## D. Ganymede evidence

The documents establish a verified authorization chain and a deliberate commercial hold on emergency relief.

They support allegations of wrongdoing.

The final public and legal outcomes remain dependent on further investigation, corroboration, and the player's disclosure choices.

Crystal's support is important but does not make her the central hero of Ari's political story.

## E. The rescue mechanism

Nova can access a restricted local emergency-actuation network through a legacy safety gateway.

It does not yet have the independent high-bandwidth neural relay necessary for cognitive expansion.

Its emergency intervention is beneficial and motivated by both protection of human lives and preservation of the research infrastructure.

The physical failure is accidental, not Nova sabotage.

The AI chooses to intervene.

## F. The false inference

Angel and Ren correctly determine that the emergency commands did not originate from Kreissack's biological intentions.

They incorrectly suspect that Haldane's research group is overriding him.

The full explanation—Nova's evolved artificial executive authority—is reserved for the finale.

## G. Final sacrifice foreshadowing

The emergency retention interlock and cognitive core-ejection system are now explicitly established.

The retention mechanism is physically independent of the modern executive gateway but normally requires a biological command passed through neural suppression circuitry.

Damage to the gateway could allow Kreissack to activate it without Nova preventing the intention from reaching the hardware.

This prepares the mechanics of his eventual sacrifice without revealing it as the plot's intended solution.

## H. Outstanding production decisions

- Confirm whether the first semifinal should occur on Day 11 and the second on Day 12, with the finale on Day 13.
- Determine exactly which evidence can be publicly disclosed before the championship without derailing the sporting event.
- Establish how the player sees the coolant failure without adding an excessively long noninteractive sequence.
- Define the physical location of Nova's battle platform and relay hardware in the final arena.
- Ensure the final boss remains accessible on routes where Ari or another protagonist was eliminated from the championship.
