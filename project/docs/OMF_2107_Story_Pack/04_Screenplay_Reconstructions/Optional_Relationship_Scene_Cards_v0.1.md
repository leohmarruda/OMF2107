## A. “The Proper Question” — Ari and Leena, Chapter 1/2

**Entry:** after Ari criticizes the Ganymede anniversary ceremony. Optional encounter; no existing romance assumed. Leena is acting as an independent journalist.

The crowd near the press balcony is thinning. Leena has paused a recording, although the red light on her own note-taking tablet is still visible.

**LEENA:** You've accused a company of choosing a contract over the lives of colonists. Do you have a document, or do you have a story?

**ARI:** I have both. Neither fits in a thirty-second clip.

**LEENA:** Good. I wasn't offering you thirty seconds.

**[CHOICE — HOW TO RESPOND]**

- **A. Share a verifiable lead:** “There's a relief authorization log. I can tell you where to look, not who gave it to me.” → `leena_evidence_lead=1`; `ari_leena_trust +1` if accurate. Leena independently checks it. No romantic flag.
- **B. Deflect with flirtation:** “Is that the interview, or are you asking me to dinner?” → playful tone, but Leena sets a boundary: “For now, it's an interview.” No automatic approval of a date.
- **C. Keep distance:** “I don't trust WAR, and I don't know if I trust the press.” → Leena accepts it, leaves an independently verifiable public lead. Essential evidence remains reachable through Christian, archives or Selene later.

**Later possible follow-up (proposed, Chapter 5; only if reciprocal rapport):** Leena has transferred Ganymede document handling and publication decisions to an independent colleague and disclosed her personal conflict to her editor. They meet outside the press compound.

**LEENA:** I can be interested in you, or I can be your reporter. I won't pretend those are the same thing.

**ARI:** And if the facts turn out to be worse than either of us expects?

**LEENA:** They don't become less true because I'd rather have a quiet evening.

**[CHOICE]** Pursue carefully / continue friendship / avoid conflict. No answer can buy favorable coverage or suppress bad evidence.

## B. “Paid in Full?” — Mara, Plug, and the workshop, Chapter 2

**Entry:** optional workshop scene, particularly for playable Mara or Ari. Plug and Mara are adults; Plug's exact age and romantic eligibility require project approval.

Mara's Flail hangs in a repair sling. Plug has set a damaged bracket on the table, precisely aligned beside a new one.

**PLUG:** I can replace it today, or you can fight next week with that crack pretending it's a design choice.

**MARA:** Give me the price before you give me the lecture.

**PLUG:** Price first. Lecture is complimentary.

A small list appears: parts, skilled labor, approved safety certification. No unexplained favor.

**[CHOICE FOR MARA PLAYER]**

- **A. Pay and learn:** “Show me the stress test. I want to know what I'm paying for.” → `plug_respect +1`; access to a believable hardware diagnostic clue later.
- **B. Offer a trade:** “I can recalibrate your old actuator rack after qualification. Actual work, actual invoice.” → `plug_working_alliance=1`, optional late paid engineering contract.
- **C. Decline and repair independently:** “I'll do it myself. Let me borrow your torque manual.” → Plug lends a public procedure, not an unsafe bypass. They remain professional allies.

**Only in a later optional reciprocal attraction beat:**

**PLUG:** It's infuriating how often you show up needing something expensive.

**MARA:** It's infuriating how often you're right.

They share a laugh. The romance can be proposed later only if Mara is free to choose, Plug's preferences are established, and no repair or equipment access is conditioned on a date. If the option is declined, their technical support still operates normally.

## C. “Off Air” — Lyra and Alex, Chapter 2/4

**Entry:** Lyra's career has been discussed as a scandal rather than as an elite pilot's. Alex has been professionally cautious about questions on her personal life. The scene is available regardless of who Lyra dates.

The broadcast lights turn dark. Alex is unpinning their microphone when Lyra enters the empty staging corridor.

**LYRA:** Am I famous because I fight, or do I fight because I'm famous?

**ALEX:** That depends which producer you ask.

**LYRA:** I'm asking the one who watched my last three championships.

**ALEX:** You cut your recovery time after every missed counter. You don't need me to certify that you earned this.

**LYRA:** Then say that on air.

**ALEX:** I already did. The network cut half the sentence.

**[CHOICE FOR LYRA PLAYER]**

- **A. Demand accurate coverage:** Lyra asks Alex to correct a misleading segment. → `alex_professional_respect +1`, creates independent media correction opportunity.
- **B. Share private fatigue:** Lyra admits she no longer knows which smiles are genuinely hers. → `alex_personal_trust +1`; friendship becomes possible without romance.
- **C. Flirt:** “You're more interesting without a camera.” → Alex can respond with a boundary or interest as separately authored; this does not create immediate dating status.

**Potential later follow-up:** Alex initiates an outside-the-arena conversation only after they and Lyra mutually express interest. If romance follows, Alex must formally recuse from coverage involving Lyra; another presenter narrates relevant match/interview scenes. Lyra cannot earn better ratings or match outcomes through the relationship.

**ALEX:** I don't want you wondering whether every kind thing I say is something I'm about to broadcast.

**LYRA:** Good. I'd hate to have to fire my favorite commentator.

**ALEX:** For the record, you don't get to fire me.

**LYRA:** That's the first thing anyone has said to me all day that I believe.

## D. “Safety Is Not a Personality Type” — Cossette, Ibrahim, Ren, and Angel, Chapter 3

**Entry:** optional dinner/technical conversation, not a flirtation option. Cossette and Ibrahim are in an established mature relationship. Ren's pronouns remain they/them in this draft.

At a quiet table overlooking the machine shops, Ibrahim gestures at a model of a high-bandwidth interface. Cossette removes its power cell before the simulation can activate.

**IBRAHIM:** It was only a projection.

**COSSETTE:** Your projections have a reputation for becoming someone's weekend emergency.

**REN:** The efficiency gains are measurable.

**ANGEL:** Efficiency at doing *whose* task?

**IBRAHIM:** The question is whether the technology can be made safe.

**COSSETTE:** And whether people can refuse it without becoming obsolete.

Ibrahim pauses. He puts his hand over Cossette's; she lets it remain there, then points to the model again. They can disagree and still be affectionate.

**[CHOICE FOR SELECTED REN OR ANGEL]**

- Seek independent replication and a pause in testing.
- Defend strictly voluntary research with disclosed uncertainty.
- Refuse personal experimentation but help analyze existing noninvasive data.

Every route earns access to a testable, *non-romance-gated* technical anomaly. If Ren and Angel are flirting, keep it in a **separate later scene**, not as payment for accepting medical risk.

## E. “The Document You Didn't Burn” — Raven and Crystal, Chapter 4

**Entry:** Crystal and Raven reunite privately after an independent investigation points to an incomplete Devroe shuttle record. Compatible with their unresolved past; no assumption of Raven's exoneration.

**CRYSTAL:** You knew there was a second dispatch log.

**RAVEN:** I knew one existed. I didn't know what was in it.

**CRYSTAL:** You waited ten years to tell me the difference.

**RAVEN:** Yes.

**CRYSTAL:** That's an answer. It isn't a defense.

**[CHOICE — IF PLAYING RAVEN]**

- Provide a verifiable custody trail and admit the extent of concealment. → unlocks full evidence examination; trust may or may not recover.
- Tell a partial truth to preserve his reputation. → Crystal independently uncovers gaps; intimacy blocked or damaged.
- Withdraw and send the documents to Christian without claiming forgiveness. → evidence can still surface; no romance entitlement.

**[CHOICE — IF PLAYING CRYSTAL]** Continue investigation together / keep him at professional distance / request that Christian oversee disclosure. Romance only emerges from an additional, separate mutual decision. Winning the tournament does not make Raven innocent.

## F. “The Only Person I Don't Need to Beat” — Dante and Steffan, Chapter 5/6

**Entry:** independent of whether their competitive showdown occurred. The feelings remain ambiguous in default canon; the **romance variation is a new optional proposal**.

A public display replays their old training footage. Steffan reaches to turn it off. Dante beats him to the button.

**DANTE:** We spent so much time trying to win, I forgot what we were arguing about.

**STEFFAN:** I didn't. I thought that if I won, you'd finally look at me without that expression.

**DANTE:** What expression?

**STEFFAN:** Like I was someone you cared enough to hate.

A long silence. Neither man can use Lyra as a pretext now.

**[CHOICE FOR SELECTED PLAYER]**

- Name the genuine hurt and apologize without conditions → mutual respect, possible friendship.
- Admit a confusing attachment and ask whether the other wants to explore it → **potential** reciprocal romance, if explicitly authored and both available.
- End the rivalry without further closeness → dignified separation.

Steffan's apology to Lyra and Dante is not a mechanism for earning either person's forgiveness or romantic availability. If one character refuses romance, the other responds respectfully and keeps his independence.

## G. “More Than a Prize” — Ari and Mara, Chapter 6 aftermath

**Entry:** fires only if actual match results, Chapter 3 relationship choice and neural program flags permit. May occur whether Ari is champion or eliminated; dialogue avoids asserting who won.

The arena is finally quiet. Mara stands beside a window overlooking a repair yard.

**MARA:** Mom's care coordinator called. We're changing the payment schedule. Doesn't solve everything.

**ARI:** Do you want me to do something?

**MARA:** Ask me tomorrow. Right now, I want to know whether you're going to tell me the truth about what happened in that lab.

**[STATE-DEPENDENT REPLY]**

- **Lovers, truthful:** Ari explains participation/symptoms or refusal/observed anomalies. Mara says she can disagree without abandoning him. No automatic breakup.
- **Lovers, concealed side effects:** Ari admits concealment; Mara can request time apart, forgive after action, or end the relationship. A win cannot buy forgiveness.
- **Friends:** Ari offers listening, not a romantic appeal. Mara appreciates it; they make plans as friends.
- **Separated:** They acknowledge remaining care without implying reconciliation, unless a separate mutually authorized later choice is offered.
- **Mara player, single:** Mara speaks with her mother or Plug instead; her future is about her own decisions, not Ari's absence.

**MARA (shared close):** We'll figure out what comes next. But I'm not handing you the steering wheel.

**ARI:** Wouldn't dream of it.

**MARA:** You absolutely would.

## Implementation gates / edge cases

1. **Never** let a nonromantic response secretly become flirtation in a later chapter. Use tone-conditioned lines, not default kiss staging.
2. If Ari is dating Leena, the press evidence has an independent verification chain; if not dating, the same lead can be published.
3. If Lyra does not date Steffan, call him a rival or acquaintance, never her ex. If she stays with Dante, the story needs its own genuine negotiation scene rather than a missing breakup.
4. If Lyra and Steffan do not meet in the actual semifinal, don't show a replay of her defeating him; instead reference the actual bracket and give Lyra a separate agency moment.
5. If Mara dates Raven or Plug in a selected-pilot route, suppress contradictory Ari romance scenes unless her prior relationship with Ari ended respectfully.
6. If Angel and Ren stay friends, do not withhold key evidence or necessary HAR assistance behind a romantic flag.
7. If Jean-Paul pursues Varga, keep Varga accountability and participant consent as independent story questions.
8. Cossette/Ibrahim continuity remains affectionate even if neither appears in the player's optional scene queue.

## Production notes
