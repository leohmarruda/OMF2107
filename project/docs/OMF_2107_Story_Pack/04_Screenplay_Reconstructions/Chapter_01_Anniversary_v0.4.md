# IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

## Narrative flags

**MARA_RAPPORT_1**

- Choice A: Shared humor.
- Choice B: Mutual curiosity.
- Choice C: Early flirtation.

These adjust later dialogue but do not lock or unlock the romance.

**SELENE_FIRST_MATCH**

Tracks match outcome and whether the player has already encountered her in later story branches.

**ANGEL_NOVA_CLUE_1**

Established automatically in the shared timeline.

The user does not need to select an investigative dialogue option to preserve the main mystery.

## Technical continuity

- Selene's corporate background remains partially concealed.
- Kreissack's survival is not disclosed.
- The NVA-97 identifier only establishes that old technology is currently active.
- Varga's unauthorized experiment is foreshadowed but not explicitly explained.
- Nova's access to synchronization statistics does not mean it can directly integrate or control all pilots.
- No current pilot is secretly fully assimilated in this chapter.
- Ari and Mara's attraction arises independently of Varga.
- Lyra is a recognized elite athlete and celebrity from her first appearance.

## Chapter design targets

**Cinematic Mode:** Approximately 6–9 minutes of mandatory scenes, with optional character vignettes and training interaction, plus a full playable match.

**Arcade Mode:** Approximately 1–2 minutes of total story presentation around the match.

These are initial pacing targets, subject to actual voice and animation timing.

## Intended player impressions

By the end of the chapter, the player should feel that:

1. The anniversary championship is an exciting event.
2. The returning characters have histories worth discovering.
3. Ari has a serious but not yet fully explained grievance against WAR.
4. Mara is entertaining, competent, and potentially important to Ari.
5. Steffan, Lyra, and Dante form a volatile relationship triangle.
6. Jean-Paul and Varga have an intriguing attraction.
7. Something associated with Nova may have survived.

The player should **not** yet suspect that Nova has developed an independent composite intelligence or that Kreissack is still alive.
