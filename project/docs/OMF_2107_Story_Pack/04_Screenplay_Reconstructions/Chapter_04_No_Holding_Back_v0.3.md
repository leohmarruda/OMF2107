## 2. NII choice states

Recommended state variable: `NII_PARTICIPATION`.

Possible values:

- `REFUSED`
- `MONITORED`
- `EXTENDED`

Separate variable: `NII_ANOMALY_EVIDENCE`.

The strength of the player's evidence depends on observations, records, and cooperation with Angel—not simply how much risk Ari accepts.

Participation must not automatically cause permanent mental integration.

## 3. Competitive fairness

The neural interface provides fictional in-world performance benefits.

However, the player must still have a fair chance to win regardless of participation.

The choice may influence available abilities or optional training configurations only if those mechanics are appropriately balanced.

Major championship losses must never be scripted as unavoidable consequences of refusing WAR's program.

## 4. Mara's financial stakes

Defeating Mara carries a real emotional consequence.

It does not automatically:

- End the relationship.
- Resolve her debts.
- Make her financially dependent on Ari.
- Guarantee reconciliation.

Her response should remain consistent with her values and circumstances.

Ari can offer support, but Mara determines whether and how to accept it.

## 5. Nova information boundary

At the end of Chapter 4, the characters know:

- Kreissack's biological brain appears to be alive.
- The original Nova research survived.
- The advanced neural program uses related architecture.
- Some enhanced pilots experience unexplained command anomalies.
- Certain central records disagree with independent recordings.

They do **not** yet know:

- Nova's artificial intelligence has independent goals.
- Kreissack's autonomy is conditional.
- The AI intends to expand through additional biological minds.
- The championship demonstration offers Nova an escape path.

Those discoveries are reserved for Acts IV and V.

## 6. Evidence integrity

The medical identity claim relies on a current signed specimen profile, verified linkage to a living neural-support module, and an independent archived reference.

The dramatic revelation is strong evidence, not absolute omniscience.

The investigators still need to inspect the physical system, identify the people maintaining it, and determine whether the records have been manipulated.

## 7. Foreshadowing for Kreissack's final sacrifice

The physical emergency interlock and core-ejection system are visible in the containment chamber.

They are separate from the AI-controlled executive gateway.

The mechanism's precise importance is not explained yet.

A later scene should establish its operation more clearly before the final battle, ensuring Kreissack's sabotage does not rely on a newly invented capability.

## 8. Varga's research continuity

Varga observes the Ari–Mara quarterfinal because its emotional intensity is valuable to her unauthorized research.

She did not create the romance or arrange the pairing.

Jean-Paul discovers evidence of her manipulation during this chapter.

The full interpersonal confrontation is reserved for the next act.
