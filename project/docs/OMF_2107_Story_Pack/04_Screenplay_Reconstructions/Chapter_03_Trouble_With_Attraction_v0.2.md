# ONE MUST FALL 2107
## Chapter 3 — The Trouble with Attraction

**Screenplay:** v0.2 (integrated optional branches; original dramatic spine retained)  
**Timeline:** Tournament Days 5–7  
**Player character:** Ari Solano  
**Mandatory championship fights:** None; concluding qualification matches are shown through broadcasts  
**Optional gameplay:** Ari vs. Mara, practice sparring  
**Chapter climax:** Ari and Mara choose romance *or* friendship; the knockout bracket is about to be revealed

### Dramatic objectives

1. Complete the group-stage standings consistently with the revised tournament bracket.
2. Make Ari and Mara's developing love feel earned.
3. Establish the difference between Steffan's genuine attraction to Lyra and his desire to humiliate Dante.
4. Show Lyra choosing Steffan and accepting responsibility for hurting Dante.
5. Make Varga's intervention meaningful but limited.
6. Introduce evidence of deliberate delay in Ganymede's emergency response.
7. Show both the promise and the disturbing anomalies of advanced neural control.
8. End with Ari facing a choice between personal trust, competitive advantage, and institutional access.

# SCENE 1 — A SMALL MISTAKE

**[BROADCAST CINEMATIC — DAY 5, MORNING]**

**INT. CHAMPIONSHIP ARENA**

Ren Akiyama's Helix stands opposite Mara Voss's battered Flail.

The enormous display shows their records.

**MARA VOSS — 1 WIN**

**REN AKIYAMA — 1 WIN**

Both have already defeated Raven.

Whoever wins takes first place in Group B.

**ALEX (BROADCAST)**

One of these pilots is going straight into the knockout stage as a group winner.

**LEENA**

And we have a fascinating contrast. Ren has some of the best recorded reaction times in the tournament. Mara has spent years fighting people who were faster, stronger, and better equipped.

**ALEX**

You're suggesting experience might beat technology?

**LEENA**

I'm suggesting Mara knows what to do when it does.

The fight begins.

Ren moves with remarkable precision. Helix anticipates Flail's opening attacks and counters with minimal wasted motion.

Mara changes tactics, repeatedly presenting awkward, imperfect openings.

Ren responds flawlessly to the first two.

On the third, Helix begins a counterattack before Mara has completed the feint.

Its movement is fast—almost too fast.

Mara abruptly reverses direction and catches the robot off balance.

Flail lands the decisive blow.

**SHIRRO**

MARA VOSS TAKES GROUP B!

The crowd cheers.

**[POST-FIGHT — COMPETITORS' AREA]**

Ren approaches Mara.

**REN**

That last feint was extraordinary.

**MARA**

Thanks. I was hoping you'd fall for it.

**REN**

I didn't.

**MARA**

Your robot did.

Ren hesitates.

**REN**

That's the strange part.

**MARA**

What is?

**REN**

I remember deciding to wait.

Mara looks back toward Helix.

**MARA**

Well, whoever decided to attack owes you an apology.

Ren laughs, though the expression doesn't quite reach their eyes.

Across the room, Ari watches the exchange.

For the moment, it seems like a curious malfunction rather than evidence of anything sinister.

# IMPLEMENTATION AND CONTINUITY NOTES

## A. Definitive group-stage results

| Group | Result 1 | Result 2 | Result 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Ari defeats Selene | Crystal defeats Selene | Ari vs. Crystal: player-dependent |
| B | Mara defeats Raven | Ren defeats Raven | Mara defeats Ren |

| D | Dante defeats Jean-Paul | Angel defeats Jean-Paul | Angel defeats Dante |

Default Group A winner: Crystal. Alternate: Ari.

Other group standings remain unchanged in Ari's route.

## B. Bracket integrity

With the established seeding convention:

- Group A winner faces Group B runner-up.
- Group A runner-up faces Group B winner.
- Group C winner faces Group D runner-up.
- Group D winner faces Group C runner-up.

The semifinals pair the two A/B quarterfinal winners and the two C/D quarterfinal winners.

Consequently, if Ari wins Group A, a later Ari–Mara meeting is possible in the semifinal, not the quarterfinal.

No match is secretly reassigned merely to maximize romantic drama.

## C. Romance continuity

At the beginning of the chapter:

- Dante and Lyra are together.
- Steffan is actively pursuing Lyra.
- Ari and Mara are attracted to each other but not yet lovers.

By the end:

- Lyra has ended her relationship with Dante and begun exploring a relationship with Steffan.
- Dante is hurt and angry.
- Ari and Mara have become lovers in the default romantic route (or remain close friends if the player chose Choice 5-B).
- Jean-Paul and Varga's mutual attraction has intensified.
- Varga has collected emotional data and begun arranging encounters.

Lyra does not yet know the full extent of Steffan's motives or Varga's role.

She therefore does not break up with Steffan in this chapter. That occurs in Act IV before their semifinal rematch.

## D. Varga's causal role

Varga:

- Has access to relevant research schedules and some facility monitoring.
- Can manipulate meeting opportunities.
- Can obtain security footage through authorized or improperly acquired access.
- Facilitates the scandal leak through an intermediary.
- Collects emotional and synchronization data.

She cannot:

- Make Lyra desire Steffan.
- Control Dante's feelings.
- Force Ari and Mara to fall in love.
- Determine tournament results.

Her actions are unethical but do not erase other characters' responsibility for their decisions.

## E. Neural-system mystery

Ren experiences an advanced-assistance anomaly.

Angel discovers an unexplained alteration in the central diagnostic record.

Neither is evidence that Ren has already been incorporated into Nova's composite intelligence.

The anomaly may result from predictive assistance crossing the boundary between anticipating and initiating motor commands.

The distinction becomes more important when Ari makes the neural-program choice.

## F. Ganymede evidence

Ari's newly acquired authorization log establishes:

- Emergency cargo was prepared.
- Departure authorization was held.
- The internal timeline contradicts the public account.
- The hold passed through commercial operations.

It does not yet establish:

- The identity of every responsible official.
- The full political motivation.
- The causal contribution of each delay to individual deaths.
- The complete approval and cover-up chain.

Those discoveries remain for Act IV.

Recommended state variables:

- `ARI_MARA_ROMANCE`: friendship / attraction / lovers / estranged / reconciled.
- `ARI_MARA_TRUST`: distinct from romance.

- **Other pilot routes:** The group-finishing matches become playable when the selected pilot participates in them.

The game should not force additional tournament matches merely to manufacture action.
