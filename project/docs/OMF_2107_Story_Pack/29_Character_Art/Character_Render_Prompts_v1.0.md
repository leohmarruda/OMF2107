# OMF 2107 — Character Render Prompts v1.0

**Purpose:** Production-ready prompts for rendering the core character cast of *One Must Fall 2107*.

**Scope:** 12 playable pilots + 12 major supporting characters = 24 characters. *(v1.1: added Tessa Kade, a recurring named character with her own romance-flag branch in Ren's campaign who had no render prompt in v1.0. The 12 pilot HARs — Jaguar, Katana, Shadow, Pyros, Chronos, Gargoyle, Thorn, Electra, Shredder, Mirage, Helix, Flail — remain out of scope for this character-render set; see `Character_Bible_v1.0.md` for their names/traits, and the suggested HAR-cockpit deliverable in section 5 below.)*

**Intended use:** Base character renders / sprite foundations for story mode, VN-style scenes, key art development, and expression-sheet generation.

---

## 1. Shared art direction

Use this shared instruction at the start of every prompt unless intentionally overridden:

> Create a **full-body character render** for a **futuristic fighting-game story mode** set in **2107**, using a **polished semi-realistic anime / high-end game illustration style** with **clean linework, cinematic shading, strong silhouette readability, high facial detail, and premium sci-fi costume design**. Show the character in a **front three-quarter standing pose**, centered, with a **neutral transparent background** or plain studio backdrop, suitable for cutting into dialogue scenes. Keep the design visually consistent across the whole cast. Emphasize **personality, clothing silhouette, age, posture, expression, and color palette**. Avoid chibi proportions, caricature, or overly generic cyberpunk clutter. The result should look like a **playable character render or dialogue sprite base**.

### Standard technical notes

- Full body visible.
- Hands visible unless impossible.
- Neutral or lightly character-defining expression by default.
- No text, no watermark, no logos.
- Costume and styling should feel like **late-21st-century corporate-glam / athletic sci-fi / industrial frontier**.
- Preserve age readability.
- Use transparent background if the renderer supports it.

### Optional suffix for sprite-base generation

> Keep the pose readable and relatively neutral so the render can serve as a base for expression variants. Prioritize a clean silhouette, clear costume layers, and unobstructed facial features.

### CHR-CRY — Crystal Devroe
**Prompt:**
Create a full-body render of **Crystal Devroe**, a **33-year-old legendary tournament heroine**. She is **elegant, athletic, confident, and composed**, with **long, straight, bright yellow-blonde hair, blue eyes and fair skin**, a poised stance, and the aura of a public icon who still feels most comfortable in training spaces. Dress her in a **practical but sophisticated futuristic outfit** that combines **deep red, white, and metallic accents**, balancing interview-ready polish with combat-athlete functionality. She should look like a veteran champion and independent public figure, not a naive rookie. Use a calm, dignified expression and subtle heroic presence.

### CHR-RAV — Raven
**Prompt:**
Create a full-body render of **Raven**, a **36-year-old dangerous antihero and former mercenary bodyguard**. He is **tall, lean, imposing, and severe**, with a controlled, predatory stillness. Likeness to the original game: dark skin, long straight black hair with a blue sheen falling over one side of the face, narrow pale eyes, a severe closed expression. ### CHR-MAR — Mara Voss
**Prompt:**
Create a **full-body character render** of **Mara Voss**, a **34-year-old underground champion, experienced pilot-mechanic, and hard-lived fighter**, for a futuristic fighting-game story mode set in **2107**.

Use a **polished semi-realistic anime / high-end game illustration style**, with clean linework, cinematic shading, detailed textures, strong silhouette readability, and realistic anatomy. Maintain visual consistency with the rest of the character cast.

**Physical appearance:**
- **Athletic but relatively slender build**, with natural feminine curves and moderate muscle definition. She should look physically capable and conditioned, but **not excessively muscular or bulky**.
- **Moderately full breasts**, slightly larger than average, with natural proportions and a subtly emphasized bust beneath her fitted clothing.
- **Short, tousled dark brown hair**, slightly messy and practical.
- Attractive but rugged facial features, with a confident, slightly irreverent smirk.
- Lightly tanned skin with subtle freckles, a small facial scar, minor scratches, and signs of a demanding working life.
- A few understated tattoos, particularly on her forearms.

**Clothing and equipment:**
- A **weathered brown leather pilot jacket**, partially open, with rolled-up sleeves, worn stitching, patches, and a raised collar.
- A **fitted, slightly dirty grey tank top** that follows her natural figure and bust without looking excessively revealing.
- **Tight-fitting dark tactical pants**, closely following the hips, thighs, and legs while remaining practical for combat and mechanical work. Include reinforced knees, subtle wear, repaired seams, and restrained red accents.
- Heavy, worn leather combat boots with practical straps and buckles.
- Fingerless mechanic gloves, utility belts, a compact tool pouch, and a thigh-mounted repair kit.
- Pilot goggles resting around her neck.
- Small improvised technological accessories suggesting she maintains, repairs, and modifies her own equipment.

**Personality and pose:**

Mara should project **toughness, independence, resilience, and irreverent confidence**. She is an experienced underground fighter who has survived difficult circumstances through resourcefulness and determination.

Her posture should feel **grounded, relaxed, and formidable**, with her weight shifted slightly onto one leg and her hands resting casually near or inside her pockets.

Her expression should combine a subtle, challenging smirk with observant eyes, conveying both **hard-earned cynicism and buried warmth**.

She should look distinctly less glamorous than celebrity competitors, but possess a rugged, natural attractiveness.

**Composition and background:**
- Full body visible, including both boots and hands.
- Front three-quarter standing pose.
- Centered composition with a clean, readable silhouette.
- **Transparent background (PNG with alpha channel)**, appearing white when displayed against a white surface.
- No scenery, text, logos, watermarks, or decorative borders.
- Suitable for use as a **game dialogue sprite, character selection portrait, or promotional character render**.

**Overall visual identity:** A rugged, attractive, self-reliant underground champion with a feminine but functional silhouette, fitted tactical clothing, weathered pilot equipment, and the unmistakable attitude of someone who has fought and repaired her way through life.
