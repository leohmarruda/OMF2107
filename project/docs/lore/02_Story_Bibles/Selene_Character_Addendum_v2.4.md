# Selene Vale — playable character addendum v2.4

**Status: editorial development, not automatically established canon.** Based on the user-established Selene outline in `Character_Bible_v1.0.md` and `Master_Story_Bible_v2.7.md`.

## Existing character foundations

Selene is a 32-year-old, highly capable Mirage pilot, heiress of a rival robotics conglomerate, and publicly an independent professional executive. She is witty, socially confident, ambitious, glamorous and adept at deception. She enjoys sophisticated rivalry with Milano and Jean-Paul, but is not inherently disloyal to allies. She wants respect for her own ability instead of wealth or inheritance, and tends to treat vulnerability as a negotiating weakness. Her legal tournament qualification and fight results must be valid even when her family ownership becomes known.

## Proposed dramatization

Her skill is **seeing institutional incentives**. She understands why money flows through subsidiaries, when business secrecy protects legitimate innovation, and when it hides misconduct. This lets her contribute a financial provenance trail to the Nova investigation without replacing Ren and Angel's scientific evidence, Mara's safety testimony, Leena's reporting, or Christian's later Devroe authentication.

Her weakness is **mistaking bargaining power for personal autonomy**. When scared, she hides behind cultivated ambiguity, polished seduction or legal instruments. Her deep conflict is with the family identity that makes her privileged and trapped at once. She may choose a credible independent firm, transparent reform from within, or ruthlessly exploiting a crisis. Each option deserves consequences rather than a simple moral scoring system.

## Character relationships

- **Milano:** mutual professional recognition, competition, flirtation and possible romance; he has his own commitments, ethics and decisions. Potential intimacy is not a joint venture or an obligation.
- **Jean-Paul:** two people with defensive sophistication may learn sincerity together; optional romance only after his Varga entanglement is independently resolved. Professional collaboration and rivalry are valid alternatives.
- **Ari:** uncomfortable but useful mirror: he says what he means; he can be a principled collaborator without romantic subtext.
- **Crystal:** mutual athletic respect; Crystal challenges Selene's reliance on performance and image.
- **Leena:** a journalist who won't exchange publication for friendship or trade secrets; Selene can contribute auditable public financial records.
- **Cossette and Ibrahim:** technical expertise and skeptical mentorship; Selene earns trust through genuine engineering respect, not purchase.

## Romance, sensuality, and restraint

Suggested fanservice includes a formal dance, rooftop poolside tension, and athletic Mirage rehearsal; intimacy is optional. The dramatic erotic tension is in their power negotiation, the brief voluntary decision to stop competing, and Selene's willingness to be seen without a role. All romantic partners are adults, mutually consenting and independent; neither corporate access nor scandal suppression can serve as a price of affection. Her default is **single**, with meaningful professional/friendship outcomes.

## Fixed timeline

Default Group A: loses to Ari and Crystal; no QF. Selected-player alternatives are possible but match outcomes remain decisive. The A/B semifinal is Day 11 **before** the Day-12 rescue; Day 13 reveals Nova's executive veto and ends with unique core destruction; the signed Kreissack Devroe murder order is independently authenticated Day 30. Her historical family records do not establish Nova was responsible for the 2092 murders.
