# Raven–Crystal canon clarification — v1.4

**Date:** 2026-11-01  
**Authority:** Explicit user characterization update superseding any older assertion that Raven is *necessarily* gentle, protective, or scrupulously self-restrained with Crystal.

## Established psychological facts

1. Raven is a genuinely morally ambiguous, dark character with a **taste for exploitation and interpersonal power**. He **enjoys the power of exploiting Crystal**, specifically her reflexive submissive response. It is not merely a form of care he awkwardly expresses, nor a misunderstanding for the screenplay to solve automatically.
2. Crystal experiences an involuntary submissive attraction to Raven. This attraction is **real and her own**, not created by Nova, Varga, incapacitation or a neural implant. It does **not** make her consent to manipulation, interference with her family, threats, lies, sexual activity, or blanket orders.
3. Raven can feel genuine affection, desire, pride, insecurity, and loyalty while also enjoying coercive leverage. His mixture of traits need not resolve into a reliably benevolent dominant. If a player chooses exploitation, the narrative must show the concrete consequences for Crystal, not reclassify them as her secret wish.
4. An elective, adult **consensual** D/s relationship is an available **conditional possibility**, not Raven's natural entitlement. It requires demonstrable conduct that respects active, specific, revocable consent and independent Devroe evidence disclosure. His dark drive survives even if he chooses self-restraint. Other routes may end in estrangement, manipulation exposed, legal scrutiny, or limited professional cooperation.
5. **Raven enjoys the exploitable imbalance in every route; Raven's actual conduct branches.** The earlier Crystal POV draft's polite, attentive Raven belongs to one possible branch. Older blanket language like "Raven listens to limits, stops promptly" is now **a requirement for eligible intimate scenes**, not an unconditional descriptive fact about him.

## Continuity and ethical distinctions

- Erotic tension can be dark without making abuse into valid consent. If he tries to isolate her from Christian, exploit the Devroe shuttle truth or pressure her into intimacy, label that *coercive conduct*, record it, and let Crystal reject and report it. No sexual payoff, secret romance score, or trophy rewards it.
- Crystal's confidence, strength and autonomy are never retroactively falsified by her desire to submit privately. Her attraction remains even when she rejects Raven; the story does not punish that refusal.
- Raven's exact shuttle responsibility and the causal facts of Crystal's parents' deaths remain unresolved. His willingness to exploit her cannot itself prove guilt for their deaths.
- Proposed Mara romance, other pairings, scene details, endings, and legal results remain editable proposals. Their independent agency and prior relationships continue to apply.
