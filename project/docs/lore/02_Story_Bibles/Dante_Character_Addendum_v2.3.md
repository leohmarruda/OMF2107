# OMF 2107 — Dante Vey characterization addendum v2.3

**Status:** Author-established psychological direction (first section) and editorial proposals (later sections). Supersedes descriptions of Dante as merely an emotionally reactive foil in the original Character Bible. Does not overwrite the author-established Steffan direction from v2.1.

## Established

1. Dante (28) is more emotionally mature, self-aware and perceptive than Steffan. This is relative maturity, **not serene wisdom** or immunity to jealousy.
2. He is a passionate, fiercely competitive Shredder pilot, loyal when secure, capable of strong anger and purposeful spite.
3. Steffan's pursuit of Lyra—genuine attraction mixed with a calculated desire to overpower Dante—angers and humiliates him. Dante may respond vindictively rather than morally admirably.
4. Dante eventually confronts Steffan and exposes what Steffan refuses to admit: Steffan's fixation goes well beyond sports, and Lyra has become a vehicle for expressing power over Dante while concealing attraction and envy.
5. Steffan remains genuinely brilliant and competent; Dante respects his talent even when enraged. Steffan's attraction to Lyra is not false. Lyra makes her own decisions.
6. **Now author-established (v2.3):** Dante's **default** feelings toward Steffan are nonromantic. He recognizes Steffan's fixation, respects his athletic talent and may be angry or spiteful; none of these imply concealed reciprocal attraction. The player can **change the course** over time through choices that open the possibility of Dante newly developing romantic feelings. In the alternate route these are an acquired, consciously examined response, not a retcon revealing that Dante always secretly desired Steffan.

**Still not established automatically:** Lyra's later romantic response, Steffan's independent readiness for a healthy new relationship, Mari's romance or Dante forgiving Steffan. Every such development needs separately authored choices.

## Dramatic model

- **Insight without control:** Dante can be correct about Steffan's motivations and wrong about how to act on them. His empathy can collapse into a wish to humiliate someone at the precise point of vulnerability.
- **Competitive integrity:** Dante treats a victory as meaningful only if the fight is real; the player cannot gain wins by punishing Steffan in dialogue.
- **Accountability without romantic reward:** Steffan's admission of desire is not the same as apologizing to Lyra, and neither requires Dante to reciprocate.
- **Private confrontation by default:** Dante exposes the truth **to Steffan** in a private Day-11 confrontation. A player-selected public escalation is harmful and changes trust, but the default narrative does not publicly disclose a presumed sexual identity.
- **Restraint as choice rather than default personality:** `merciless`, `clear`, `vulnerable` are all coherent reactions. His better moments do not erase genuine capacity for spite.
- **Lyra is neither a possession nor a bargaining chip:** Her prior romance, departure, and final athletic victory are not caused by Dante's insight; Varga's study influenced encounters without manufacturing feelings or rigging matches.

## Relationships

**Lyra:** They knew one another before the tournament, developed a romantic relationship early, and arrive at the Day-1 gala already together (author-established). Initial established partnership; she may choose to leave Dante for Steffan (default), remain with him (alternate), or become single. His willingness to accept her decision influences trust, not her right to choose. Reconciliation is possible only after time and independent reciprocal decision.

**Steffan:** Emotionally loaded rivalry; Steffan's attraction to Dante is established on Steffan's side, not Dante's. Dante's perception is perceptive, not supernatural; he adduces Steffan's habitual monitoring of Dante, his focus on Dante's reaction to Lyra, and his behavioral hesitation. Optional future mutual romance is only one of several endings, conditioned on time, accountability, availability, mutual choice, and no unresolved coercion.

**Mara:** Mutual fighting respect and a civilian-safety alliance, not an automatic romance.

**Mari Solis (proposed new adult supporting character, 31):** Shredder contractor who independently owns maintenance observations and can teach Dante emotional patience without being his therapist. She may be a close friend or mutually interested romantic prospect if contract conflict is resolved. Never withholds safety data until Dante dates her.

**Shirro, Leena, Angel, Ren:** Interact through media ethics, independent investigations and the climax. Their evidence does not depend on Dante defeating Steffan or pursuing any partner.

## Signature exchange

**DANTE:** “You wanted me to look at you. So you made her into the message.”

**STEFFAN:** “And what is it you want from me, Dante?”

This question should complicate Dante as much as it exposes Steffan. Do not require the screenplay to answer it with a mutual romance.

- Dante Day-11 confrontation: `DV5.2` and Steffan optional/default observed counterpart `ST5.1b`.
- If Lyra never dates Steffan, Dante can still confront Steffan about attempting to use her and manipulating publicity; no fabricated adultery or breakup.
- Steffan's Day-12 `ST5.4` is an optional follow-up reflecting the result of the Day-11 confrontation, not their first encounter or a repeated revelation.
- Do not replace Lyra's independent protected Day-11 receipt of Varga findings with Dante's perception: different kinds of evidence, both important.
- Default sports: Dante beats Jean-Paul, loses to Angel, loses Q3 to Steffan; Steffan later loses semifinal to Lyra; Ari beats Lyra in final. No match draws.

## v2.3 — Default / elective romance precedence (author-approved)

- **Default route:** After the Day-11 private confrontation Dante does not experience romantic attraction to Steffan; he can maintain distance, form a professional truce, or reluctantly respect him as a rival. Their shared history is emotionally intense without being a love story.
- **Player-divergent route:** Respectful curiosity during early training → optional open listening at the Day-11 confrontation → explicit voluntary exploration after the Nova crisis and a meaningful interval. Dante can **begin** to experience and recognize attraction through this process. Earlier curiosity and listening by themselves are not an attraction declaration.
- **No inevitable revelation:** Avoid writing Dante's default epilogue or earlier jealousy as evidence of secret desire. Steffan's side of the fixation is unchanged.
- **No forced response:** If Dante explores, Steffan must separately acknowledge his feelings and meet accountability/relationship gates. A player selecting `steffan` does not control Steffan's consent, repair of harm to Lyra or athletic results.
- **No romance requirement:** Nonromantic friendship, truce, estrangement and solo endings are narratively complete and do not withhold mystery evidence or the Nova climax.
