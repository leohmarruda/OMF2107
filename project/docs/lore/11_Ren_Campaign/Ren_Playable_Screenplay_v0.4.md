# ONE MUST FALL 2107 — REN AKIYAMA: THE UNFINISHED SELF
## Six-chapter playable cinematic screenplay · v0.1 · 2026-11-01

**Status:** Complete first-pass *editorial proposal*, not approved final canon. Ren is **25**, uses **they/them**, and pilots **Helix**. This is Ren's independent player-selected campaign; the original Ari, Lyra, and Mara campaigns remain the authority when those characters are selected. New civilian character **Tessa Kade (30)** and all optional romances remain proposals.

**Reading key:** `MATCH` = actual playable, decisive fight (one winner, one loser); its result is obtained from the tournament engine. `[IF]` = conditional scene; `CHOICE` writes a persistent narrative variable. `SAFE CLUE` = essential information available regardless of romance, neural participation, tournament elimination, or dialogue. `OPTIONAL` = skippable without losing that information. All sensual/romantic interactions are adult, mutual, and opt-in; private intimacy fades to black. **No romantic or neural choice grants combat statistics, alters a match result, or substitutes for an opponent's consent.**

**Ren's default non-player timeline:** Beats Raven, loses to Mara, qualifies second in Group B; loses the quarterfinal to Crystal after unusually strong synchronization and an anomalous neural event; helps Angel and Crystal investigate Kreissack's survival and Nova. When Ren is selected, only Ren's actual victories and defeats determine advancement. **Official tournament and later Nova confrontation are separate.**

**Core states:** `research_path` = `withdraw` / `audited` / `persist_safeguarded`; `symptom_disclosure` = `full` / `limited` / `concealed` (can be revised, with consequences); `evidence_method` = `offline_record` / `witness_protocol` / `hardware_isolation`; `connection` = `angel` / `tessa` / `friendship` / `single`; `consent_clearance` = earned independently of the character's feeling of attraction. All relationship intentions remain proposals until reciprocal choices are authored. Ren's experimental symptoms warrant independent examination whichever route the player chooses.

**SAFE CLUE R3-K1:** Every branch gains a verified mismatch between one command-authorization timestamp and Ren's independently recorded intention signal.
