# OMF 2107 — Milano Angston: THE PRICE OF CONTROL

**Playable cinematic screenplay · first editorial draft v0.1 · Story Pack v2.5**  
**Six chapters · Days 0–13, with an optional Day-30 epilogue.** Milano Angston is **45**, a WAR board contender and pilot of **Gargoyle**. The narrative describes *one selected-player timeline*, not all protagonists' victories occurring simultaneously. Scenes bearing `[CONDITIONAL]` replace, not supplement, contradictory scenes. Adult romance and glamorous fanservice are optional, suggestive, and fade out; any unapproved new detail is a proposal rather than established canon.

**On-screen fight order:** Milano fights **Lyra first (`GC3`)**, **Steffan second (`GC2`)**. The common tournament simulator consumes Milano outcomes in `GC2`, `GC3` order; the route adapter converts `(beat_lyra, beat_steffan)` to `(beat_steffan, beat_lyra)`. Default Milano loses both, finishing third in Group C. The provisional 1–1–1 published-seed rule eliminates him if he beats Steffan but loses to Lyra; beating Lyra alone advances him second. No fight ever draws. Nondefault NPC knockout outcomes remain provisional, not canon.

**Shared timeline:** Kreissack's biological survival is independently established on **Day 9**; Nova's **genuine** life-saving intervention and false attribution occur on **Day 12**; its covert executive veto, Kreissack's final independent action, and destruction of the **unique composite core** occur on **Day 13**; Christian independently authenticates Kreissack's signed 2092 Devroe murder order on **Day 30**. Milano cannot obtain that authenticated evidence earlier. Kreissack's final spoken line is **“Apparently, I can still surprise you.”** The Nova fight is an emergency, separate from the sporting bracket.

**MILANO:** “Power isn't the problem. It's who gets to exercise it.”
