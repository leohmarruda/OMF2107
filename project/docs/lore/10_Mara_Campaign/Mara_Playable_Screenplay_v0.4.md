**Status:** First-pass playable-POV screenplay, **editorial proposal**, not automatically approved canon. For selected Mara only. The v0.6 Ari chapters and v0.7 Lyra campaign remain the authority for those selected pilots. **Mara: 34, Flail.** All prospective romantic participants must be verified consenting adults; Plug's exact adult age, romantic orientation and mutual preference remain to be approved. Scenes labeled `OPTIONAL` can be skipped with no loss of required evidence, tournament progress, or access to Nova.

**Play contract:** Every official fight has exactly one winner. Mara's actual two group results and conditional quarterfinal, semifinal and championship results come from the tournament simulator. The film must read opponent names, qualification, standings, and the official champion from state rather than assuming the default. **Match IDs are NOT chronological sequence numbers:** default dramatic scheduling shows Mara–Raven (`GB2`) before Mara–Ren (`GB1`); the simulator's input order is `[vs Ren, vs Raven]`.
