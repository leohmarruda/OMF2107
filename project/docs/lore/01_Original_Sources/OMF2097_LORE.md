# One Must Fall 2097 — Baseline: Original Plot and Characters

**Purpose:** a reference of what the original game (Diversions Entertainment, 1994) establishes, kept separate from any proposed expansion. Use it to check whether a story element is canon or new.

**Reliability labels**

- **(+)** supported by the original manual, original game data, or a source listed at the end. Prefer the local manual where it covers the claim.
- **(~)** taken from the OMF3K treatment or a secondary source but not yet checked in the original manual or game data.
- **(-)** not established in the original; do not treat as canon.

Community wikis are low-reliability sources ⚠️. Where I rely on one, it is marked.

---

## 1. The premise

- **(+)** The setting is 2097. World Aeronautics and Robotics (WAR) holds a stranglehold on the space-travel business and is the most powerful entity on Earth.
- **(+)** A new, powerful post has opened: WAR's representative to Jupiter's newly colonized moon, Ganymede.
- **(+)** Ten corporate applicants each have their own reasons and motives. WAR judges them equally qualified, so they settle it by fighting in robots.
- **(+)** The robots are Human Assisted Robots (HARs), operated through a physical and mental link with a human pilot (manual, printed pp. 2–3).
- **(+)** The manual presents the pilots as physically safe from robot-on-robot combat (printed p. 3).
- **(+)** The manual profiles ten selectable pilots and eleven HARs, including secret Nova (printed pp. 23–32). It describes five arenas; every arena except the Stadium has hazards (pp. 20–21).
- **(+)** Tournament Mode is a separate career mode in a later HAR-sport setting. Its custom pilot earns prize money, trains, upgrades a machine, and climbs the rankings (pp. 15–22). The Ganymede contest is the premise of One Player mode. The OMF3K treatment keeps these timelines separate.

## 2. The central mystery: Nova and the Devroe parents

- **(+)** Crystal and Christian Devroe are twins. Their father, Dr. Devroe, was a head researcher at WAR five years before the story. His reputation is what got the twins their positions.
- **(+)** His research was highly secret. Crystal knew only that it related to biogenetic research.
- **(+)** Both parents died five years earlier as the only passengers on a shuttle from Luna to Earth. Christian suspects murder and keeps his suspicions to himself. Crystal suspects it too.
- **(+)** Crystal's only clue is a DigiLink access code marked "Nova," found in her father's briefcase.
- **(+)** She believes winning Ganymede would give her access to WAR's Core computer system, where the code might open the secret files. She believes WAR is responsible.
- **(+)** Major Hans Kreissack is WAR president and in charge of the secret Nova project (manual, pp. 27, 32). **(~)** His role as the final opponent piloting Nova comes from secondary accounts and should be checked against the game's ending and fight data.
- **(~)** Nova is Kreissack's project to keep a human brain alive in a robotic body; he uses it on himself. The Devroe parents were killed after opposing it.
- **(~)** Iron Fist is tied to the Devroes' murder.
- **(~)** Nova's research is lost: the lab burns, and Kreissack dies in the final fight. ⚠️ A fan-site summary also describes the Nova labs as destroyed by a fire whose cause is unexplained.
- **(~)** Each pilot has a distinct ending. The text of individual endings still needs verification in original game data.

The manual establishes motives for Raven, Milano, Ibrahim, Jean-Paul, Shiro/Shirro, and Steffan. Angel's origin and motive remain intentionally unclear there. Use the manual profiles for those distinctions before adding story material.

## 4. What is *not* canon

These appear in the attached treatment as proposals. Do not treat them as original:

- The deep-link system, the concealed medical effects, and the altered medical reports.
- Earth's charter officials and the government cover-up.
- Pilot response maps as training data for Nova.
- Any romantic or sexual relationship between pilots.
- The named anchor and reporters, their personal arcs, and player-directed relationships with them.
- Spiritual, womanizer, or terrorist motives assigned in the OMF3K adaptation where the original manual does not state them.
- The specific route choices and outcome table.
- Any mechanism by which WAR's contest doubles as a weapons or recruitment trial.

## 5. Open questions worth checking before treating anything as final

1. The exact text of each pilot's ending (the decoded in-game text).
2. Each pilot's exact HAR assignment in original game data; the manual presents pilots and robots separately.
3. Whether later game text reveals more of Angel's background than the manual.
