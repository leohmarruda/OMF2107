Where they disagreed, the original screenplay text was kept as canon; the reconstructed editions contributed later decisions (Lyra's semifinal win, Group D, final Ari–Lyra) and the Repair Pack patches (see the `[REVISION]` markers in Chapters 3, 5, 6 and the appended section in Chapter 4).
