# OMF 2107 — Christian Devroe: **The Space Between the Records**

**Format:** Six-chapter **supporting-character parallel screenplay** (not an eighth playable pilot, and not a tournament competitor). Embedded or optional scenes may be shown in any of the seven playable campaigns. All quoted lines and choices below are new editorial proposals, not historical 1994 OMF canon.

**Narrative contract:** Christian, 33, is Crystal's twin, a former competitor and independent investigator/security specialist. His parents died on a Luna–Earth shuttle in 2092. His investigation is his own, not delegated by Crystal, not purchased by Raven, and not extinguished by the player's romance choices. He can be mistaken, controlling or afraid while remaining a competent investigator. Raven's guilt and the independent evidence follow `Devroe_Shuttle_Case_Canon_v1.5.md`. The senior assassin principal is **Kreissack** in v1.7 canon, but Christian does not receive independent proof until E5 on **Day 30**.

**Shared chronology:** Day 0–1 opening; Days 3–5 qualification; Days 6–8 E2 relay; Days 8–10 E3 lethal-command forensic report; Days 9–11 E4 restored-channel request receipt; Day 11 first semifinal; Day 12 genuine Nova rescue followed by second semifinal; Day 13 championship and later Nova special confrontation; weeks later independent oversight hearing. Fight victories and neural choices elsewhere do not modify the 2092 facts. The 2107 Nova override motive becomes provable only after the official final.

**Christian's dramatic question:** *What if keeping someone safe means accepting that you cannot choose for them?* A public hearing can correct a record, but cannot bring back his parents, make Crystal stop wanting Raven, or return the last fifteen years. The story must offer Christian a future beyond investigator and brother.

## CHAPTER ONE — THE EMPTY SIGNATURE (Days 0–1)

### CH1.1 — Anniversary reception / the empty place

**INT. WAR ANNIVERSARY EXHIBITION — NIGHT**
**[BG: BG-SOC-GALA@NIGHT]**
**[MUSIC: MUS-GALA | behavior=palette; continue_same_theme_unless_edited]**

Giant screens replay Crystal's 2097 victory. Below them: a polished display of historical competitors, the dead, and the heroes who supposedly brought the age of secrecy to an end. Christian carries an invitation he did not want to use. The camera stays with him rather than Crystal when a reporter calls her name.

A curator invites him to sign the exhibition guest book under **CRYSTAL DEVROE — FAMILY**. He crosses out the last word.

**CHRISTIAN:** I'm not a family member of a trophy.

**CURATOR:** We have a separate registry for former competitors.

**CHRISTIAN:** Good. Use that one.

He has trained as a fighter; he can admire her achievement. He is tired of being asked to certify the success of a system that left his parents' case closed.

At a side archive terminal, a civilian shipping index displays a maintenance transfer stamped *CUSTODY COMPLETE*. The corresponding receiving authorization is blank. He photographs the discrepancy and its catalogue reference, not the raw contents of a protected database. It is a lead, not yet proof of sabotage.

**CRYSTAL:** [PERF: tone=resentful | expression=EXP-BITTER | delivery=clipped | intensity=1] You never come to these things for the speeches.

**CHRISTIAN:** Neither do you.

**CRYSTAL:** Mine pay for the catering.

**CHRISTIAN:** Then they can afford to tell the truth on the placards.

She offers to introduce him to the event's public records officer. He wants to refuse because she suggested it. He takes the name anyway.

**Optional player-facing interaction (Crystal POV, `C1.3` neighborhood):**
- **Investigate together:** Christian shares catalogue ID and civilian custody question; they disagree about whether to confront Raven now.
- **Parallel investigations:** Two separate custodians preserve records, allowing fewer conversations and less family friction.
- **Ask a neutral official to coordinate:** Christian consents to a shared contact but retains control of his own witness list.

*All three leave E1 independently accessible by Day 5. None means Crystal has to let Christian manage her private relationships.*

## CHAPTER THREE — THE HOLD (Days 6–8)

**[MUSIC: MUS-BANTER | behavior=palette; continue_same_theme_unless_edited]**

Two independent time sources occupy a wall: the insurer's spool and a station technician's sealed work order. **OFFICER:** He could say the designation was legitimate.

**CHRISTIAN:** Then we compare it with the licensing authority that should have issued it.

**OFFICER:** No such license exists.

**CHRISTIAN:** That's the conclusion I want in your words, not mine.

**CRYSTAL:** You didn't say "I told you so."

**CHRISTIAN:** It's taking everything I've got.

## CHAPTER FOUR — THE OTHER CAUSE (Days 8–10)

### CH4.1 — Flight trajectory reconstruction

**INT. TECHNICAL REVIEW HEARING — DAY 10**
**[BG: BG-INV-HEARING@DAY]**
**[MUSIC: MUS-AFTERMATH | behavior=palette; continue_same_theme_unless_edited]**

The independent engineer reconstructs a deliberate flight-control override; the contractor's maintenance trail has an Iron Fist-linked intermediary. The senior signer and the exact person who typed the command are **not yet proven at Day 10**. E3 is authenticated; E5 arrives only on Day 30.

**CHRISTIAN:** So the blackout caused the collision?

**ENGINEER:** No. The flight-control command caused the fatal deviation. We can show the blackout existed, and that it removed an emergency channel. We cannot show the crew would otherwise have survived.

He wants to ask again and make the answer different. He does not.

**CHRISTIAN:** Then put that in the final report. Exactly that.

A public statement which exaggerates Raven's conduct would be easier to repeat than the forensic finding—and more vulnerable to challenge. He chooses the harder version. Crystal, when present, decides her own response. Scene aligns with canonical **D2**.

## Integration notes

1. **Scene ownership:** CH1–CH6 are Christian's lens, *not* a claim that the player can choose Christian as a tournament pilot. Place optional extracts in Crystal C1.3, C3.4, C4.3, C5.1B, C6.4; Raven R1–R6; Ari's Chapter 4 Ganymede evidence scene; or supporting ensemble scenes. Do not re-stage D1–D3 as contradictory duplicate hearings.
2. **Authorial restraint:** The reader can know Raven's regret or enjoyment from Raven POV, but Christian's dialogue rests only on his own acquired records. No scene accuses Nova of 2092 sabotage or treats Varga's later neural experiments as its direct cause.
3. **Control:** The player can encourage, assist, challenge or disengage from Christian; he retains independently authored decisions and the case evidence survives refusal, early tournament elimination, romance, and neural choices.
4. **Romance eligibility:** Leena–Christian is **proposed, not established canon**. Only credible mutual choice, global exclusivity and independent editorial recusal permit it. Flirtation or a kiss at the venue is not a commitment. A romance elsewhere closes the epilogue without deleting her agency or professional work.
5. **Adult sensual tone:** Optional banter, a dance, warmth, and private kisses provide romantic fanservice without interrupting the tragedy investigation or using evidence as erotic leverage.
6. **Continuity limits:** E1 is a custody-gap finding, not proof of murder. E2 proves Raven's signed suppression. E3 proves separate sabotage and its contractor route, **not** the senior kill-order signatory. E4 proves the ignored restoration request, **not** that a rescue would have worked. **E5 on Day 30 proves Kreissack's lethal authorization; the final legal dispositions remain open.**
