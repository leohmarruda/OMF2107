# OMF 2107 — Secondary Romance Expansion v0.2

**Status:** Editorial proposal based on Story Pack v2.6. **Author-approved design decision:** Cossette and Ibrahim may explore consensual non-monogamy (CNM) while remaining a couple. Specific scene details, partners, and relationship agreements remain proposals. No active screenplay or validator modified.

## Why this is needed

The twelve pilots have first-pass relationships, but supporting adults are unevenly served: some have only professional interactions, romance exists mainly in epilogues, and a player's opportunities to form bonds can depend heavily on protagonist choice. Supporting characters should have romantic and nonromantic agency, private lives, and emotional arcs beyond supporting the pilot's investigation.

**Current baselines (do not silently overwrite):** Dr. Ilse Varga (approximately 51) has an established volatile attraction to Jean-Paul alongside serious unauthorized emotional experimentation. Alex is a nonbinary broadcast anchor of unfinalized age; Lyra–Alex is already a proposed post-tournament romance, conditional on editorial recusal. Cossette Akira (49) and Ibrahim (58) are a long-standing affectionate couple whose disagreement over neural integration is not a breakup in default canon. All newly proposed romance characters must be confirmed adults.

## Candidate route design

### 1. Dr. Ilse Varga — dangerous intimacy, not automatic rehabilitation

**Primary established thread: Jean-Paul–Varga.** Expand beyond the existing dance/confrontation to give Varga her own decisions: whether to acknowledge the relationship, surrender prohibited research records, and accept investigation independently of intimacy. Possible outcomes include affair and estrangement, a fragile post-accountability relationship, or genuine attraction with no reunion.

**Optional second thread (proposal): Raven–Varga.** They recognize in each other the pleasure and danger of psychological control. A private interaction after Raven's separation from Crystal can turn into a dark, mutually chosen attraction or a rivalry over incriminating information. It is not a guaranteed romance, does not exempt either from culpability, and must not have Varga covertly experiment on him. Why it adds value: it lets the player choose proximity to another manipulator without making them morally good or rehabilitated by desire.

**Reject as currently framed:** Varga–Ren or Varga–Angel while she has research/clinical authority over their experiments. Termination of that authority and independent ethics review would need to precede any substantially different future proposal.

**Scenes:** (A) a witty late-night laboratory conversation with Jean-Paul, moving from flirtation to a concrete choice about consent and evidence; (B) a charged Raven–Varga conversation in a sealed records room, with either party able to end the encounter; (C) Varga's aftermath, with an independent decision about the investigation no matter who—if anyone—loves her.

### 2. Alex — public composure vs. private desire

**Priority existing thread: Lyra–Alex.** Start with genuine respect amid publicity pressure; keep reciprocal attraction optional. The broadcaster must recuse themself from Lyra's coverage before pursuing intimacy, and Lyra's breakup / relationship state must be settled independently.

**Optional second thread (proposal): Dante–Alex.** After the default Dante–Lyra separation, Alex sees the unusually private, angry side of Dante that never appears in interviews. The emotional question: can Dante be vulnerable without converting the relationship into another contest with Steffan? He can choose friendship instead, while Alex may decline the romantic path. Alex must cease reporting on Dante and have no editorial leverage in the private relationship. This is exclusive with Dante–Lyra and elective Dante–Steffan outcomes.

**Scenes:** (A) late-night unrecorded conversation after a misleading broadcast; (B) nonsexual romantic tension during a gala or post-match rooftop event; (C) a career-costly recusal choice; (D) private epilogue where Alex retains an independent career whether partnered or single.

**Important:** Alex's gender identity is not an automatic romance eligibility flag. Preferences and attraction compatibility need a character-specific authored decision; neither Lyra nor Dante can unilaterally unlock Alex's affection.

### 3. Cossette — mature intimacy and a fully independent personal life

**Default established thread: Cossette–Ibrahim.** Develop their actual romance, not just their role as a comforting background couple: technical disagreement, humor and affection, an optional grown-up sensual interlude (non-explicit), a strained evening after a dangerous research decision, and a jointly or individually chosen post-crisis future. Neither paralysis nor age removes Cossette's desirability or agency; avoid turning disability into a source of automatic tragedy or 'rescue' romance.

**Elective expansion (author-approved framework; pairing still proposed): Cossette–Milano within Cossette–Ibrahim CNM.** Cossette and Ibrahim remain an established, affectionate couple. They may independently choose to open their relationship through an explicit, mutually desired agreement, without separating or portraying one as insufficient for the other. Cossette–Milano is a possible downstream relationship, not an automatic reward for choosing CNM. Its appeal comes from technical respect, institutional disagreement and attraction; Milano can also say no. Record Milano's relationship with Selene separately: no overlap unless *every* affected partner has voluntarily agreed to a compatible structure. WAR leadership, funding, and employment conflicts require independent treatment.

**Scenes:** (A) a technical workshop dinner where Cossette and Ibrahim argue, then reconnect; (B) a private, warmly flirtatious scene showing their enduring physical intimacy without explicit detail; (C) an optional private discussion in which **both** freely consider CNM and define expectations (no player-forced opening); (D) a Cossette–Milano conversation about technical ethics and mutual attraction, only after CNM eligibility is established; (E) a follow-through scene testing how their agreement works when jealousy, scheduling, or conflicts of interest become real; (F) Cossette acting as engineer, friend, or whistleblower regardless of romantic outcome.

## System and continuity rules

- **NPCs are agents, not prizes.** Advance a romance only if both parties' independently maintained choices, availability and attraction are compatible.
- **Global relationship ledger:** record `participants`, `interest`, `relationship_status`, `commitment_type`, `start_day`, `end_day`, `consent_or_mutual_choice`, and any necessary `professional_recusal` and conflict-resolution events. Keep one canonical record shared across POVs.
- **Three levels of connection:** optional flirt/fanservice scene; relationship development; distinct sustained romance ending. Not every flirt should create a new complete ending.
- **Competing partners:** current Varga–Jean-Paul, Lyra–Alex, Dante–Lyra/Steffan, Crystal–Raven, Milano–Selene and Cossette–Ibrahim paths must be reconciled at the timestamp of each event. Compatibility is a matter of *agreed relationship boundaries*, not an automatic rule that all romances are exclusive. Avoid instant breakups simply to make NPCs available.
- **Evidence independence:** romance cannot grant sole access to vital proof; Varga's ethics investigation and Nova/Devroe evidence continue even if all romance options are refused.
- **Fight independence:** no relationship or fanservice choice rewrites recorded match outcomes.
- **Agency and intimacy:** distinguish consensual erotic tension from deliberate manipulation or coercion; neither Varga nor Raven is automatically redeemed by a romantic choice.
- **Production:** each route needs a friendship/nonromantic counterpart, scenes with concrete choices and later consequences, and cross-POV tests. Romance additions should not overload the main six-chapter cinematic pacing.

## Cossette–Ibrahim CNM route: implementation sketch (v0.2)

**Stable baseline:** Together and affectionate. The story never treats Cossette's disability or age as a rationale for opening the relationship. CNM is a considered preference rather than a cure for a dysfunctional relationship.

1. **Conversation:** Cossette expresses curiosity; Ibrahim responds as a person with his own preferences. They can decline, defer, or both opt in. Nonromantic protagonists may witness the consequences but cannot decide for them.
2. **Agreement:** If both choose CNM, establish scope and practical safeguards—disclosure expectations, safer-sex discussion, discretion around the tournament, time allocation, and whether other partners may also have partners. These are negotiated and revisable rather than universal rules for CNM.
3. **Potential attraction:** Cossette and Milano may flirt at a professional event. The route advances only if Cossette and Milano reciprocate and any existing Milano–Selene commitment is compatible or resolved honestly.
4. **Consequences:** Include positive everyday intimacy *and* a real challenge, such as a misunderstanding about publicity or jealousy, resolved by conversation rather than a contrived infidelity plot.
5. **Endings:** (a) Cossette–Ibrahim remain together without outside romance; (b) both enjoy an agreed CNM arrangement, whether or not Cossette dates Milano; (c) they remain together but close the arrangement if **both** elect to renegotiate. A breakup should never be an automatic punishment for CNM.

**Suggested state fields:** `cossette_ibrahim_status=together`, `cnm_discussed`, `cossette_cnm_choice`, `ibrahim_cnm_choice`, `cnm_agreement_active`, `cnm_boundaries_recorded`, `milano_interest_cossette`, `cossette_interest_milano`, `milano_other_commitment_compatibility`, `professional_conflict_resolved`. Never infer consent from an attraction flag or a flirt scene. Neither Cossette nor Ibrahim loses their separate professional story if CNM is not pursued.

**Validation cases to add when implemented:** reject unilateral opening; reject coerced or uninformed consent; reject Milano overlap without compatible status for Selene; allow negotiated CNM with no secondary partner; ensure CNM and non-CNM branches preserve the same tournament and indispensable Nova evidence.
