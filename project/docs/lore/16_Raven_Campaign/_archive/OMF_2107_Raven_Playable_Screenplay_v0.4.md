# ONE MUST FALL 2107 — RAVEN: THE PRICE OF CONTROL

**Selected-pilot screenplay — six chapters, draft v0.2 (1 November 2026)**  
**Project baseline:** Story Pack v1.3, shared event ledger v1.1, established Raven/Crystal history and Devroe shuttle evidence ambiguity.  
**Status:** **Author-established:** Raven is morally ambiguous and possesses serious dark traits. He **enjoys the power of exploiting Crystal's involuntary submissive response to him**; this is not merely a misunderstood protective instinct. His desire can coexist with affection, and his capacity to choose otherwise does not erase the pleasure he takes in leverage. **Also established:** Crystal's particular involuntary submissive attraction to him. **Draft proposals:** individual acts of manipulation, their consequences, Raven's legal fate, specific evidence devices, optional Mara attraction, and all new dialogue. The Devroe shuttle case is resolved as **new v1.5 working sequel canon** in the case bible: a human contractor sabotaged the flight; Raven knowingly enabled an unlawful relay hold, ignored a restore request and hid records. Specific verdicts remain open.

## Play contract

- **Raven (36), Katana.** Former bodyguard for Kreissack; capable fighter, ruthless observer, skilled in coercion, compartmentalization, and self-justifying bargains. He initially wants money and freedom from WAR's old command structure. His objective can become possession of leverage, concealment of old crimes, genuine independent accountability, or a compromise that corrupts both.
- **Crystal (33), Jaguar.** The former champion, active investigator and public figure. Raven knows she feels compelled to yield to him, and **likes** knowing it. Her attraction is involuntary; her actions and relationship decisions are not. Sometimes she can be manipulated into a nonsexual decision, and that must be legible as a wrongful use of pressure, not as consent or proof that exploitation is romantic. She can catch the manipulation, refuse him, tell Christian, investigate anyway, or leave. If genuine private D/s intimacy is chosen, it must be knowingly negotiated and must not purchase silence, money, evidence or exclusive control over her life.
- **Combat:** On-screen Group B encounters are **Mara (`GB2`) then Ren (`GB3`)**. Engine ordering happens to match this screen order: (`GB2`, `GB3`). Default losses to both. A sole win over Mara leads to third place; a sole win over Ren leads to provisional B2; both wins earn B1. QF: B2 → `Q1` versus A1; B1 → `Q2` versus A2. Semifinal only if QF won (`S_AB`, Day 11); final only if SF won (`FINAL`, Day 13). No match draws. The true final winner takes the trophy, whoever it is. Nondefault NPC knockout picks and a three-way circular group ranking are explicitly **provisional design placeholders**.
- **Evidence:** Devroe shuttle custody manipulation is independent of the sexual/romantic route. Kreissack biologically alive is established in Chapter 4. Nova's genuine accident rescue occurs Chapter 5, Day 12 midday, **after** Raven's possible S_AB. The AI's covert executive veto, conditional brain integration and autonomy agenda are not revealed until Chapter 6 after the real championship final. Nova's emergency encounter is not a match in the official bracket. Ganymede human wrongdoing and Varga's psychological study remain separately accountable.
- **Morality is not a numerical meter.** Each important behavior changes concrete records, witnesses and trust. A player can choose cruel or predatory behavior without the script presenting it as admirable, and a late apology cannot erase Crystal's experience. The opening characterization persists even if Raven ultimately restrains himself.
- **Romantic options:** charged but conditional Raven–Crystal; provisional, mutually independent Raven–Mara; or solitude/brief noncommittal adult flirtation. Crystal's private submissive orientation never forces her to choose him. Other characters' unavailable commitments cannot be overridden. No romance or fanservice affects match results or seals off the Nova investigation.

# CHAPTER 1 — AN OLD WEAPON
**Days 0–1 | Question: If no one can command Raven anymore, why does he still want to command someone?**

### R1.1 — The last invitation [MANDATORY]
**INT. JOVIAN ANNIVERSARY GALA — NIGHT**

The old 2097 footage fills the dome. The announcer calls Crystal Devroe *the woman who freed a generation*. The camera cuts between Crystal, in a fierce red dress, and a row of sponsors who have purchased the right to applaud her.

At the perimeter stands RAVEN, immaculate in dark formalwear, a scar visible above his collar. He is not interested in the speeches. He counts cameras, private security teams, and doors. His old employers taught him that any hall can become a trap. He learned to enjoy being the person who knows where the traps are.

A WAR liaison offers him a commemorative security contract, including a bonus for "discretion about historical matters." Raven reads the arbitration clause and folds the document once.

**LIAISON:** You must miss having a proper chain of command.

**RAVEN:** I miss knowing who paid for the chain.

`CHOICE — opening professional stance:` accept an unrestrictive consultation without surrendering records / accept the conditional fee but keep a copy of the restrictions / refuse and follow the money independently. The second creates a material liability, not a secret superpower. None decides his matches.

On stage, Crystal turns from a camera with the practiced exhaustion of someone accustomed to being loved as a symbol. Raven notices her shoulders relax a fraction when she steps outside the light. He knows that expression. He also knows how to produce it.

### R1.2 — An unpleasant discovery [MANDATORY]
**INT. BACKSTAGE GALLERY — LATER**

Crystal passes him without slowing. Raven says her name. She stops.

**CRYSTAL:** Not here.

**RAVEN:** You didn't even ask what I wanted.

**CRYSTAL:** There's a very small list.

He shifts, opening the path to the elevator. She notices that he has done so without seeming to move at all. He says, quietly, "Look at me." Her gaze rises before her expression hardens. She hates the speed of her own response.

For one second he feels a clean, terrible satisfaction: the famous champion obeyed the sound of his voice. He knows the difference between a reflex and permission. Part of him wishes he didn't.

**CRYSTAL:** Don't mistake that for an invitation.

**RAVEN:** I wasn't going to tell you what it meant.

**CRYSTAL:** You were going to enjoy it.

A pause. He does not lie.

**RAVEN:** Yes.

The answer frightens her less than it ought to. That frightens her more.

`CHOICE — Raven's reaction:` **PROBE** (tease and test how far her reflex extends; adds a *boundary-testing incident*), **WITHHOLD** (let her wonder what he knows, cultivating dependence; adds a *deliberate ambiguity incident*), or **ADMIT** (say plainly that he enjoys having power over her, without claiming the right to use it; no incident). All choices preserve his attraction to exploitation. A boundary incident is a negative record whether or not she calls it out immediately.

Crystal names what he will not be allowed to trade on: "My family. My brother. That shuttle." He smiles once, without warmth.

**RAVEN:** You're right to put them first.

**CRYSTAL:** I'm not asking for your permission.

She leaves. This time she does not look back.

**RAVEN:** She choose you to speak for her?

**CHRISTIAN:** No. She can tell you herself.

`CHOICE — evidence handling:` secure an independently verifiable copy / retain the index as personal leverage / approach a lawyer for protected disclosure. Keeping leverage delays voluntary cooperation but **cannot** eliminate Christian's independently held custody gap.

His civilian evidence is his own work; Raven's player may slow a confession, never remove the E1–E4 independent record. The two investigations may share a scene, but no Raven POV grants Christian forbidden early knowledge.

# CHAPTER 2 — TWO DEFEATS OR A WAY OUT
**Days 3–7 | Question: What is Raven worth when brute force cannot make people concede?**

### R2.1 — Mara: the refusal [MANDATORY; `GB2`]
**INT. GROUP B ARENA — DAY**

Katana enters through a narrow red light shaft. MARA's machine appears on the opposite platform, unadorned and meticulously maintained. Raven can hear the commentators trying to turn both of them into familiar villains.

**MARA, PRIVATE COMMS:** I know what you did to get that old security clearance.

**RAVEN:** Then you know it won't help me in here.

`PLAY MATCH GB2: Raven versus Mara — decisive win or loss.`

- **If Raven loses (default):** Katana strikes the barrier and drops to one knee. Mara wins cleanly and offers no pity. **MARA:** "You kept trying to scare me when you should've been reading my feet." Raven acknowledges the loss through clenched teeth; he does not claim sabotage.
- **If Raven wins:** Mara powers down without excuses. **MARA:** "Good fight. I still don't owe you anything." Raven recognizes that victory did not buy her respect or attraction.

`POST-FIGHT CHOICE:` congratulate her privately / needle her about the result / avoid her. The first can improve professional respect, the second makes any proposed romance less likely. Match outcome remains locked.

### R2.2 — The price of silence [MANDATORY]
**INT. HOTEL NETWORK AUDIT SUITE — NIGHT**

A former WAR contact offers Raven a modest fortune to certify that old shuttle records were properly destroyed. The contract contains a lethal ambiguity: it pays for *nonexistence*, not truth. If the records survive, Raven's old handling of them can be investigated.

**CONTACT:** You always understood the value of silence.

**RAVEN:** It's gone up.

`CHOICE — dirty money:` accept a clandestine advance (creates a traceable payment and future leverage against Raven) / refuse and photograph the contract / pretend to negotiate while placing a copy with independent counsel. Even if Raven takes money, the player cannot make independent evidence cease to exist. This is a morally costly branch, not a fail-safe solution.

### R2.3 — Ren: the machine that listens [MANDATORY; `GB3`]
**INT. GROUP B ARENA — NEXT QUALIFYING DAY**

REN's HELIX moves with deliberate asymmetry. Raven notices a split-second delay between a pilot's tactical intent and a corrective stabilization movement, but it is **not proof** of Nova's covert integration.

**RAVEN:** You're letting another person decide when to move.

**REN:** You're assuming anything I don't do myself belongs to a person.

`PLAY MATCH GB3: Raven versus Ren — decisive win or loss.`

- **If Raven loses (default):** Ren neutralizes his final feint. Raven sees the clean result and says nothing about cheating. Later, privately, he asks whether the stabilization software can be disconnected.
- **If Raven wins:** He defeats Ren with disciplined timing, not insider controls. Ren accepts the result and remains willing to discuss research governance.

`CHOICE:` alert a safety auditor to an observed synchronization anomaly / seek information for leverage / tell Ren directly and accept refusal. None establishes that Ren has been fully integrated or that Varga controlled the fight.

# CHAPTER 3 — LEVERAGE
**Days 6–8 | Question: Is wanting someone the same as wanting to own their choices?**

### R3.1 — The interview he cannot control [MANDATORY]
**INT. NEUTRAL INVESTIGATION ROOM — DAY**

Crystal arrives with Christian and a neutral records officer. Raven has brought the partial index, or the damaged original if he gambled on selling it. The meeting is recorded by agreement. In the corner, the glass shows a ghostly reflection of Katana's crimson colors from the adjacent exhibition hall.

**CRYSTAL:** Tell us what you know.

**RAVEN:** Depends what you're asking.

**CRYSTAL:** I am not here to play the game you think we're playing.

**RAVEN:** That's never stopped anyone from losing it.

The neutral officer authenticates the relay suppression he personally authorized. A civilian dispatch duplicate and an insurer spool establish the same blackout; he admits or denies knowing who was aboard according to the chosen testimony, but the independent record survives. The mechanism that killed the Devroes is not yet established by **this** evidence. See D1 in `17_Devroe_Case/Devroe_Scene_Packet_v1.5.md`.

`CHOICE — testimony:` **FULL** (deliver every verified relevant index and acknowledge his knowing concealment) / **SELECTIVE** (tell provable truths while keeping a crucial personal copy) / **FALSE DENIAL** (deny participation against the record; will face independent contradiction). The investigation still obtains other chain-of-custody evidence. A false denial cannot later be treated as a harmless misunderstanding.

**CHRISTIAN:** I don't need you to be a monster to need your testimony.

**RAVEN:** You'd like that to be true.

**CRYSTAL:** I need it to be true.

### R3.2 — Crystal's reflex and Raven's choice [OPTIONAL; NOT A CONTRACT]
**INT. QUIET CONSERVATORY — EVENING**

Crystal enters freely. She has insisted the door remain unlocked. The distant music of the anniversary retrospective comes through the walls. She is dressed for a private reception, not a confrontation. Raven notices the open throat of her dress, the nervous adjustment of a glove, and her determination not to reveal the nerves.

**CRYSTAL:** I want to understand what happens around you. I don't want you to define it for me.

**RAVEN:** You know I like it when you listen.

**CRYSTAL:** I know you like it too much.

He tests the space with silence. Her eyes drop for a moment. The thrill he feels is real; he does not need to deny it to the player. He also recognizes an opening for a trick.

`CHOICE — the temptation:`

1. **EXPLOIT:** Tell her her judgment is clouded and she should postpone contacting Christian until Raven "decides" she is calm. This is a deliberate attempt to isolate her, **not consensual D/s**. For an awful moment it **works**: Crystal puts away her phone and postpones an interview with a departing security witness until morning. The witness departs and the opportunity is lost, though separately secured records preserve the central evidence. When she realizes what Raven engineered, she independently tells Christian and the investigator. Raven is privately exhilarated by the fact he could move her judgment, then confronted with the harm he caused. A manipulation incident is recorded and trust damaged. There is **no romantic reward scene** for obtaining compliance through pressure.
2. **BARGAIN:** Offer private truth only in exchange for a private relationship. Crystal refuses the condition. She may remain attracted, but evidence barter poisons any romance gate until Raven independently discloses and the incident is repaired in ways she accepts, if ever.
3. **DISCLOSE:** Admit, without asking for forgiveness, "I enjoy the part of you that answers me. I could use it against you. Sometimes I want to." Then step back and offer the evidence through her investigator. This is the only choice that preserves a credible possibility of *later* consensual intimacy, and **it does not guarantee one**.
4. **LEAVE:** End the conversation before he acts on the impulse. The attraction continues, but neither trust nor romance automatically advances.

**CRYSTAL (on EXPLOIT/BARGAIN):** You don't get to make a bargain out of my vulnerability.

**RAVEN:** I could have told you what you wanted to hear.

**CRYSTAL:** You still haven't told me what I need to know.

**CRYSTAL (on DISCLOSE):** You just made it sound worse.

**RAVEN:** It is worse.

**CRYSTAL:** Good. Then we can stop pretending it isn't.

### R3.3 — A rooftop full of people who have nothing to hide [OPTIONAL ENSEMBLE / FAN SERVICE]
**EXT. JOVIAN VIEW-DECK POOL — SUNSET**

LYRA stages a publicity event that accidentally turns into a pool party. Raven arrives in black. Lyra surveys him with open disappointment.

**LYRA:** You made a swimming pool look like a hostage exchange.

**RAVEN:** I can leave.

**LYRA:** That's how I know it's your idea of a joke.

Mara, still wearing a racing jacket over a swimsuit, beats him at a miniature Katana game and refuses his rematch. Crystal may glance at him across the pool, drawn and wary. Any Raven/Crystal interaction depends on prior conduct; if he pressured her, she avoids private conversation and speaks with friends instead. The cinematography may enjoy glamorous bodies and physical confidence without equating privacy with a lack of attraction.

`CHOICE:` be an unexpectedly good sport / trade flirty banter with an available Mara / leave for the security archive. All are optional; no essential clue is lost. If Mara has a partner or rejects Raven, the scene is friendly or sarcastic only.

### R3.4 — The argument from a dead institution [MANDATORY]
**INT. SECURITY ARCHIVE ANNEX — NIGHT**

**ANGEL:** You are not telling me what he thought. You are telling me what you learned.

### R4.3 — The shuttle index is not a pardon [MANDATORY]
**INT. DEVROE EVIDENCE HEARING — NIGHT**

**CRYSTAL:** People might. We won't.

If he previously told the truth, he can submit a sworn account and accept independent authentication. He may cooperate **now**, but it does not retroactively erase the lie.

`CHOICE — accountability:` **TESTIFY** (disclose his full verified role and accept investigation) / **LIMITED** (cooperate only on independently subpoenaed facts; civil and criminal exposure continues) / **OBSTRUCT** (attempt to suppress his portion; unsuccessful against preserved independent copies, generates an obstruction incident). **CHRISTIAN:** You want me to thank you for evidence you spent years hiding?

**RAVEN:** No.

**CHRISTIAN:** Good. At least we agree on something.

**RAVEN:** I can get you access to a private sponsor.

`CHOICE:` offer an unconditional professional referral / explore mutual attraction only if Mara independently initiates and is free / leave her to train. The former is not a shortcut to a romance. The second is provisional; Mara retains the right to say no for any reason.
