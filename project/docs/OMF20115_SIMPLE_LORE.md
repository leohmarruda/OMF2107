### OMF2115: Compact Lore & Character Focus (The Devroe Siblings)

**The Premise**
In 2097, the WAR corporation hosts a televised Ganymede representative tournament where ten pilots control giant robotic HARs via neural links. While the public sees a glamorous, risk-free contest, WAR secretly runs a faster **deep-link** ("Tier-2") that maps pilots' nervous systems to train **Nova**—a program designed by Major Hans Kreissack (103) to keep human consciousness alive inside robotic bodies for independent colony control. Repeated linking causes physical damage, exhaustion, and psychological craving. Pilots conceal their symptoms to protect their contracts, sponsors, and competitive standing.

---

### Character Focus: Crystal Devroe & Christian Devroe

#### 1. Crystal Devroe (23) — *The Seeker*

* **Public Mask:** Composed, camera-ready, and hailed as the pilot who effortlessly "handles pressure".


* **Want & Wound:** She seeks proof of what truly happened to her parents in a shuttle "accident" and wants access to the Core through Ganymede. Her wound is the trauma of that loss and being told it was an accident before anyone checked.


* **Secret & Symptoms:** She has already secretly tried the "Nova" access code once, putting her on WAR's radar. She suffers from hidden hand tremors, masked by meticulous poise and a rehearsed "nerves" excuse.


* **Arc:** Transforms from a solitary investigator demanding "proof first, consequences later" to someone who recognizes the heavy responsibility of releasing unverified evidence.



#### 2. Christian Devroe (23) — *The Shield*

* **Public Mask:** Aggression and outward confidence.


* **Want & Wound:** Seeks revenge and to keep Crystal alive. His wound stems from suspecting murder for five years while telling no one. His default path pushes him toward a dangerous, violent strike on a WAR facility—a **terrorist motivation** framed as a moral choice rather than heroism.


* **Secret & Symptoms:** He has been paying a fixer connected to the violent network Iron Fist for information, inadvertently leaking pieces of Crystal's schedule. He uses stimulants and bravado, treating physical pain as proof of winning while Dr. Varga flatters his symptoms as "exceptional".


* **Arc:** Shifts from protecting Crystal by trying to control her to trusting her and rejecting violence against civilians.



#### Their Dynamic

A tight, protective, yet fractured bond. Christian’s protective instincts and destructive default path collide with Crystal's meticulous investigation, creating mutual tension over how to handle the truth of their parents' deaths.

---

### Core Conflict & Resolution

* **The Trap:** Tier-2 enrollment is framed as a rewarding promotion, but reporting symptoms traps pilots in Dr. Ilse Varga's supplemental evaluation ward.


* **The Climax & Endings:** The conspiracy unravels through leaked medical telemetry, broadcast choices, and exposure of the Nova project. Depending on player choices, the outcome ranges from publishing everything to secure public oversight, demanding independent reform, or claiming Ganymede's mandate.
