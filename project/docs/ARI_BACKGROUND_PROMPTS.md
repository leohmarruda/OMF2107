# Ari route: background prompts

The nine story backgrounds and illustrations for Ari's Chapter 1, collected from `OMF_2107_Story_Pack/26_Background_Registry/background_states.csv`. All of them now exist in `project/renders/` (the `BG-*` files plus `art_arena_broadcast.png`, `art_nova_lab_2097.png` and `art_ganymede_colony.png`) and are built into `project/build/art` by `project/tools/build_ari_art.py`.

Character prompts live in `OMF_2107_Story_Pack/29_Character_Art/Character_Render_Prompts_v1.0.md`; the fanservice scene prompts are in [ARI_FANSERVICE_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md](ARI_FANSERVICE_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md).

## Not covered

- **HARs.** Thorn (Ari's screenplay HAR) and Mirage (Selene's) have no prompts in the pack, and the prototype only renders Jaguar, Shadow and Katana, so the fight uses those. A HAR cockpit or faceoff image still needs an author-supplied reference.
- **One-line extras** with no prompt in the pack: the sponsors' representative, the gala guest, and the technician in scene 4.
- **Ari is written masculine** in the character prompt, while the screenplay uses "they" and says pronouns adapt to the player's customization.
