OMF 2115 - story slice playtest
================================

Thank you for testing. This is an early build of a one-player story slice: pick a HAR,
read a short exchange between two siblings (Crystal and Christian), fight one match,
make a choice about some evidence, and see how it plays out. It takes about 15 minutes.

HOW TO START
  1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (not inside the zip viewer).
  2. Double-click Play.bat. It resumes where you left off.
     Play-NewGame.bat ignores the saved progress and starts over.
  Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with sound on. If Windows SmartScreen warns about the
  programs, choose "More info" then "Run anyway". Nothing is installed.

CONTROLS
  Story:   Enter / Space  advance or pick      Up / Down  move between choices
           S  skip ahead to the next choice    L  open the log of what was said
           R  restart the story                M  mute / unmute
           Esc  quit
  Setup:   Left / Right  choose your HAR, Enter to start.
  Match (a second window opens after a few seconds):
           A / D  move      W / S  jump / duck      J  punch      K  kick
           Hold away from your opponent to guard.   Esc  closes the match.
           When it ends, the story window returns on its own.

WHAT I WOULD LIKE TO KNOW
  - Did anything confuse you, break, or crash? Where exactly?
  - Did the fight feel fair and readable? Did the story window hand over to it smoothly?
  - Could you tell that your evidence choice (and the fight result) changed what came later?
  - Was the writing understandable? Was any part too long or too slow?
  - Were the sound and music at a comfortable level?
  - Anything you would change first.
  If something goes wrong, please note the time and what you were doing, and send me the
  files in project\build\story\ (your save and notes) if they exist.

THIS BUILD IS PRIVATE
  It contains artwork, sound and music from the original One Must Fall 2097, used here for
  development only and not cleared for release. Please do not share or upload it.
