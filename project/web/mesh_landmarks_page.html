How to use

Drag each point onto the matching joint in both views. A point's height is shared, so moving it up or down in one view moves it in the other; the front view sets its sideways position, the side view its depth.

Click a point, then use the arrow keys to nudge it (Shift = 5 px) in the view you last clicked. Scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan. The dark area around the picture is also draggable. "l" is the character's own left, so it is on the right of the front picture.

Where the joints are: a shoulder is where the upper arm meets the torso, not the shoulder blade or wing above it; a hip is where the thigh meets the pelvis; head_base is the underside of the helmet. The hollow rings show the generator's built-in positions: double-click a point to put it back on its ring.

Save the download as project/data/mesh_landmarks.json, then run generate_meshy_meshes.py (or run-meshy.ps1). With depths set, the generator assigns mesh vertices to bones in 3D.
